Questions advisers ask

Straight answers.

Do you guarantee new clients?

No. We don't control markets or prospects. We control inputs: traffic, education, and systems. That's why our fees are tied to actual new client households and fees collected, not vanity metrics.

Who owns the assets and the data?

You do. The funnel is operated for your firm; we don't custody or manage assets, and we don't cross-sell your prospects.

Can you work with multiple firms in my area?

One firm per metro. We don't run the same class against itself in the same market, so founding slots are allocated by geography.

What does my team actually have to do?

Three things. Record the 45-minute class and three short ad clips (about a half-day, once). Give your compliance officer a look at everything before it goes live. Take booked meetings within 48 hours. We handle the rest.

How does compliance review work?

Your CCO or compliance provider sees every page, script, email, and ad before it's used, and can red-pen anything. The class is educational only: no performance, no guarantees, no product pushes. We keep copies of everything for your books and records.

What happens at day 30?

We sit down with the numbers: registrations, show rate, qualified meetings, new households. If it's working, we continue on a simple monthly engagement with the same performance terms. If not, you keep the funnel and the data and we part friends.
