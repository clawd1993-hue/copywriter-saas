First: the 12–18 month scoreboard

By 18 months from today, your goal is: - 5–10 RIAs as paying clients - 2–3 RIAs with undeniable growth results you caused - 1–2 RIAs on "growth + upside" agreements (this is the part you asked about)

If you hit all three, you have proof you're "the guy" AND the legal plumbing to someday do a roll-up.

What "growth + upside" actually means

Plain English: You start as "just" a marketing/growth vendor. Once you've proven you work, you renegotiate with the best ones so that: - You still get paid a base fee (so you don't starve). - You also get paid a slice of the growth you create. - You ALSO get some kind of right tied to equity or exit.

That third piece is what I meant by "option / equity-flavored."

Think of it like this:

Phase 1: Normal client deal For your first 6–12 months with an RIA: - They pay, say, $15k/month + maybe some setup fee. - You install the growth system, run campaigns, build the case study. - At the end, you can clearly say, "We added ~$X in annual fee revenue."

This is where you prove you're not a clown.

Phase 2: Upgrade best clients to "growth partner" deals Now pick the top 1–2 who: - Like you - Are growing because of you - Are good potential roll-up targets long term

With those you propose a new agreement that looks something like:

Lower base fee - Example: drop from $15k/month to $7k/month. - Easier for them to say yes, shows you're confident.

Performance component - You get, for example, 10–20% of net new advisory fee revenue you generate above an agreed baseline. - So if they were doing $4M/year in fees and now they're at $5M, and you both agree that extra $1M was from your system, you might earn $100–200k of that.

Equity-flavored upside This is anything that ties you to the value of the firm, not just monthly fees. For example: - A call option: "You can buy up to 10% of the firm in the next 5 years at 8x current EBITDA." - A profit interest / phantom equity: "If the firm ever sells, you get 5% of the sale proceeds above today's valuation." - A ROFR / ROFO: "If we decide to sell a controlling stake, you get first shot to buy at the same terms any other buyer offers."

You don't need to be a lawyer right now. You just need 1–2 RIAs who have signed paper that says: - We pay you retainer + performance, and - If / when we sell or recap, you participate in the upside in some defined way.

Those 1–2 upgraded deals are the first bricks of your future holdco. They are the proof that: - Owners trust you enough to give you upside. - You can negotiate for more than just "fee for service."

How to write this as a personal 18-month target

Copy this, tweak the numbers if you want:

By Month 18: - I have 8 RIAs paying us $X/month on our growth system. - At least 3 have published case studies showing $Y+ in net new annual fee revenue we created. - At least 2 are on signed "growth + upside" agreements that include: - A lower base fee - A % of net new advisory revenue - Some contractual right tied to equity or exit (option / profit interest / ROFR).

You hit that, you're not a guy with a dream anymore. You're the growth partner with paper in place. That's when a roll-up stops being fantasy and starts being a sequencing problem.
