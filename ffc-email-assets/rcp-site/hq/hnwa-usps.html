What they actually sell

Snappy Kraken · $199 / $299 / $899 a month on annual terms + $199–499 setup. Newsletters, birthday and market-update drips, AI email builder, content library, "lead generation" campaign templates, Convos texting on upper tiers, website add-on $99–199/mo (+$499–2,499 setup), broker-dealer compliance review. No ads, no qualification, no booking. You still have to find the prospect.

FMG Suite · same category, bigger and older. Compliant content library, websites, email, social, compliant texting, a "Do It For Me" tier. Same gap: content and a website, no acquisition.

SmartAsset AMP · ~$25k/yr subscription. A consumer fills a quiz on SmartAsset, gets matched, a concierge validates and live-connects. The same lead goes to up to 3 advisors at once; whoever dials fastest wins. Their own ROI math assumes a 3–4% close rate.

Zoe Financial · no per-lead fee; the advisor pays Zoe a share of that client's revenue for 5+ years (or a large lump sum). CFP/CFA + 5-year minimum to join. Zoe owns the relationship and the brand. SEC censured Zoe in October 2026, $450k penalty, for conflict-disclosure failures (RIABiz 2026-10-28).

Datalign · WiserAdvisor · Planswell · same shared-lead model at lower price points (Planswell $450+/mo, one advisor per lead, comes with a plan).

AcquireUp / White Glove / LeadJig / TouchFuse / Acquire Direct · the seminar giant. Dinner seminars and webinars, direct mail + Facebook, venues, "Performance-Based Campaigns: pay only for households that attend." No asset qualification, no booking, no follow-up. You work the room and chase the no-shows. The Anywhere Advisor and Azella are smaller versions.

Revenx · $90–150 per pre-set appointment + $2,000 onboarding (waived on quarterly/annual). Prospect pool = state, university and federal employees; annuity/IUL slant; comes with 40+ closing-training videos. Retirement Prospects · $32 per Facebook annuity lead + $299 setup. Skyline Social · leads $5–50, self-booked appointments ~$200, advisor funds $1–3k ad spend. Qualified Appointment Setting · phones leads you already own, no public price.

Pay-for-performance agencies (closest to us, all weak): Advanced Agent Marketing (annuity-focused), Revenx, OJay Media, Clientell. Pay-per-appointment or retainer + ads; none install a system the advisor owns, none qualify on assets before booking, none price on closed households.

What we do differently

  • We qualify every lead for you. Assets ($500k+), timeline, state, before anyone at your firm touches it. Vendors send names; seminar firms send attendees; we send people who already told us what they have.
  • We book your calendar. The setter texts, answers questions, and puts the qualified household on your calendar. Nobody else ends at a booked meeting.
  • Exclusive and yours. No 3-way lead split (SmartAsset), no vendor-owned client (Zoe). The page, the list and the data belong to you.
  • Education-first, not dinner-first. A 45-minute class your retiree watches from the couch, running every week. Not a steak dinner you show up for every month.
  • We run the ads, in your ad account, with your money, visible to you. Content tools don't run ads; lead vendors keep the traffic.
  • Paid on results. No setup, no retainer, a fee per closed household with $500k+ AUM. Nobody else gets paid only when you sign the client.
  • One firm per metro. Your competitor down the road can't buy the same system from us.
  • Installed, not rented. GHL sub-account, workflows, scripts, funnel on your domain. If we part ways you keep the machine.
  • Compliance baked in. SEC Marketing Rule language in the templates; every text and call logged and exportable for your CCO.

Pricing caveat: "paid on results" is the pilot wording. Exact numbers ($1k/household + 10% first-year fees, then $5k/mo from day 30: memory/ria-offer-locked-terms.md) stay on call 2; the public line is "no cost to you, no retainers, small fee per closed client."
