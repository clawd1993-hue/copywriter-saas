4 · AI outbound calling → Retell AI + ElevenLabs voice

What it is: within 5 minutes of a registration, an AI voice agent calls the retiree, runs the same qual script by phone, books the slot on the advisor's GHL calendar, and falls back to SMS on no-answer. Only ever to people who registered on the funnel. Never cold.

Decision (2026-11-13): Retell, not GHL native

Retell AI GHL native Voice AI
Sounds human ~700ms response, handles interruptions, no robotic pause (the pause is what gives AI away, not the voice) A tier slower; fine for a demo, not for a $750k household
Voice ElevenLabs voices plug in natively ElevenLabs voices importable (agency, ≤10)
GHL integration Native, built and maintained by Retell. One private-integration token + location id (we already have both). Agent tools mid-call: Search/Get/Update/Create Contact, Add Tags (fires GHL workflows), Create Note, Create/Update Opportunity, Check Availability, Book / Reschedule / Cancel Appointment on the sub-account's calendar. Post-call: writes extraction results to custom fields, logs the transcript as a contact note. Built in, zero wiring

Verdict Use this. Premium, and it still keeps GHL as the system of record. Skip.

Ruled out: ElevenLabs Agents standalone (best voice, no GHL connection; use its voices inside Retell), Vapi (engineering, real cost $0.25–0.33/min), Bland (API-only, $0.14/min), Synthflow (annual enterprise contracts).

Integration check (Retell docs, 2026-11-13): connect = GHL Settings → Private Integrations → token with Contacts, Opportunities, Custom Fields, Calendar scopes → paste token + Location ID into Retell → Integrations → GoHighLevel. Contact sync polls GHL every 5 min, matches on phone number, writes back mapped fields, logs every conversation as a note. Our existing "Warren build" token already carries those scopes. Verified smooth: no Zapier, no middleware, no webhooks to maintain.

The rules (so this never surprises a client)

Allowed? Why
AI call to someone who registered on the funnel (consent line includes calls) ✅ Prior express written consent = the form. Call within 5 min. This is the whole play.
AI cold call to a retiree who never opted in ❌ never TCPA: $500–1,500 per call, FCC ruled AI voice = robocall (Feb 2024). The RIA gets sued, not us.
AI cold call to an RIA (B2B) ❌ Mobiles are covered regardless of who owns them; most owners answer on their cell. Human only.
Human cold call, DNC-scrubbed ✅ A person with the GHL dialer, not Retell.

How the script sounds (the "is it a bot" question)

Consent = permission to call. It does not buy the right to claim to be human. Two layers: - Federal (FCC): no upfront "I'm a bot" required. Must disclose if asked, and must never deny it. "Are you a real person?" → "I'm the automated assistant for Dennis's office, but I can book you straight in with him." A lie here is the lawsuit. - Disclosure states (California, Utah, a handful more): one-clause upfront disclosure by law, regardless of consent. Script applies it automatically from the contact's state.

Default opener (~45 states):

"Hi Jane, it's the concierge from Dennis O'Keefe's office, you just registered for the retirement class. Got a sec?"

Disclosure-state opener:

"Hi Jane, it's Dennis O'Keefe's office, this is our automated assistant giving you a quick call because you just registered for the retirement class. Got a sec?"

Half a sentence, said like a human, then the same conversation. Retirees hang up on dead air and robotic pauses, not on that line. Design target: sounds human, never claims to be.
