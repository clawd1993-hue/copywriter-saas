4 · AI outbound calling (the research)

What it is: within ~5 minutes of a registration, an AI voice agent calls the retiree, runs the same qual script by phone, books the slot, and falls back to SMS on no-answer.

The field, checked 2026-11-13

Tool Quality / latency GHL integration Price (real-world) Verdict
GHL Voice AI (native) Mid. Fine for "did you register, want to book?" calls. ElevenLabs voices can be imported (agency-level, up to 10). Built in. Outbound from a workflow, books GHL calendars, custom actions mid-call. Zero wiring. $97/mo unlimited per location (fair use) or pay-per-use (engine + TTS + LLM) Start here.
Retell AI Best overall for production call automation in 2026 reviews. ~700–800ms latency, strong outbound, branded caller ID. ElevenLabs voices supported. Native, maintained by Retell: reads/updates contacts, opportunities, custom fields, writes notes after calls. Setup = private integration token + location id. Pay-as-you-go from ~$0.07/min; ~$0.10–0.15/min all-in Upgrade path if native isn't good enough on client #1.
ElevenLabs Agents Best raw voice quality. Webhooks/tools only. You build the GHL wiring yourself. Minute bundles Use its voices inside GHL or Retell. Don't run it standalone.
Vapi Developer toolkit, full control. GHL "tool provider" (get/create contact, check availability, create event). Advertised $0.05/min, real $0.25–0.33/min Engineering project. Skip.
Bland High-volume dialer. API + webhooks, middleware needed. ~$0.14/min Skip.
Synthflow No-code, enterprise. Marketplace app, calendar import, pushes call data to workflows. Annual enterprise contracts only now Skip for a 3-firm pilot.

Decision

  1. Phase A (pilot): GHL native Voice AI, ElevenLabs voice imported, triggered from the setter workflow 5 min after submit if no SMS reply. One platform, one snapshot, nothing to wire.
  2. Phase B (after hearing it live on client #1): if latency or voice quality costs bookings, switch the call step to Retell. Native GHL integration, same number, same workflow, same custom fields. Half a day to swap.
  3. Never: raw ElevenLabs Agents, Vapi, Bland, Synthflow. Either engineering or contracts we don't need.

The rules (so this never surprises a client)

Allowed? Why
AI call to someone who registered on the funnel (consent line includes calls) ✅ Prior express written consent = the form. Call within 5 min. This is the whole play.
AI cold call to a retiree who never opted in ❌ never TCPA: $500–1,500 per call, FCC says AI voice = robocall (Feb 2024). The RIA gets sued, not us.
AI cold call to an RIA (B2B) ❌ Mobiles are covered regardless of who owns them; most owners answer on their cell. Human only.
Human cold call, DNC-scrubbed ✅ A person with the GHL dialer. Not Retell.
Agent opens with "I'm a bot" Not required federally Opens like a concierge: "Hi Jane, it's the concierge from Dennis's office, you just registered…"
Agent asked "are you a real person?" Must say no FCC: disclose when asked. "I'm the automated assistant for Dennis's office, but I can book you straight in with him." Never deny it.
Upfront AI disclosure Required in some states (CA, UT, others) One-line opener, applied per contact state.

Design target: sounds human, never claims to be.

5 · AI email setter

What it is: the same Conversation AI agent answers and nurtures by email, in-thread, from a sending address on the client's domain. For leads who register but don't text back, and for the preview RIAs who reply to Michael's outreach by email.

  • Email sending domain authenticated in the sub-account (Settings → Email Services → add domain, SPF/DKIM). Previews: hnwacquisition.com. Clients: a subdomain on their domain (mail.firm.com) — Warren, DNS by client
  • Turn on the Email channel in the Conversation AI agent (AI Agents → Conversation AI → Agent → Channels → Email); plain-text replies, 2–5 min delay, signature as the concierge — Warren
  • Email-specific guard rails: never quote fees, never send attachments, one question per email, hand off to Michael on "who are you" — Warren
  • Workflow: registrant with no SMS reply in 24h → email 1 (class link + one question) → AI continues the thread — Warren
  • For RIA prospects (outreach replies): separate agent "HNWA Outreach" that only books Michael's 15-min, never pitches — later, after the first closes

Cost: inside the AI plan; email sends ~$0.001 each. Done when: a reply to a setter email gets an on-brand answer in the same thread within 5 minutes.
