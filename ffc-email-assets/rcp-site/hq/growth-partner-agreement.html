This is a high-level commercial structure for a "Growth + Equity" agreement you'd upgrade a winning RIA client into. Not legal advice. A lawyer turns this into real paper.

I'll write clauses in normal language, and in CAPS IN [BRACKETS] I'll spell out "what you get."

1. Purpose & Parties

"We agree you are not a generic agency. You are our Growth Partner responsible for designing, installing, and operating our client acquisition and conversion system in exchange for fees + performance upside + exit upside."

[THIS MAKES IT CLEAR YOU'RE MORE THAN A VENDOR, WHICH JUSTIFIES PERFORMANCE + EQUITY]

2. Term & Scope

  • Initial term: 3 years, auto-renew yearly unless either side gives 90 days' notice.
  • Scope: you own strategy and execution for a defined set of growth functions (e.g. paid media, funnels, nurture, tracking, basic sales process).

[THIS LOCKS YOU IN LONG ENOUGH TO MATTER AND MAKES YOU "THE" GROWTH ENGINE, NOT A SWAP-ABLE AGENCY]

3. Compensation – Base + Performance

3.1 Base Retainer - They pay you a reduced fixed monthly fee that covers your team and tooling.

[YOU GET PREDICTABLE CASHFLOW SO YOU'RE NOT EATING WHAT YOU KILL EVERY MONTH]

3.2 Performance Fee on Net New Advisory Revenue - Define a Baseline Period (e.g. last 12 months of fee revenue). - Define "Net New Advisory Fee Revenue" as: current trailing-12 fee revenue minus baseline, adjusted for market performance and acquisitions. - They pay you X% (say 10–20%) of Net New Advisory Fee Revenue attributable to your system, calculated quarterly with a clear formula and data source.

[YOU GET A PERCENTAGE OF THE GROWTH YOU CREATE, SO YOUR UPSIDE SCALES WITH THEIR AUM/FEES]

4. Equity / Exit Upside

4.1 Performance Warrant / Profit Interest - On signing, you receive a contractual right that if the firm is sold or recapitalized within, say, 7 years, you get Y% (for example 5–10%) of enterprise value above today's agreed valuation, paid in cash or rollover equity.

[YOU GET A SLICE OF THE EVENTUAL SALE VALUE OVER TODAY'S LEVEL, WITHOUT PAYING CASH NOW]

4.2 Call Option on Minority Equity (Optional Extra) - You may buy up to Z% equity at a pre-agreed multiple (e.g. 8–10x current EBITDA) any time before a sale.

[YOU GET THE RIGHT, NOT THE OBLIGATION, TO "BUY IN CHEAP" BEFORE A BIG ROLL-UP MULTIPLE]

4.3 Right of First Refusal / Offer - If they get a third-party offer to buy control, you can match the economic terms for your pro-rata piece or participate in the new HoldCo on similar terms.

[YOU DON'T GET CUT OUT WHEN A BIG BUYER SHOWS UP; YOU CAN JOIN OR MATCH THE DEAL]

5. Data, Reporting & Attribution

  • They must give you full access to CRM, custodian reports, fee billing, etc.
  • You jointly sign an Attribution Schedule: how baseline is set, how new revenue is calculated, when it's reviewed.

[YOU GET THE DATA NEEDED TO PROVE YOU DROVE THE GROWTH AND TO CALCULATE YOUR CUT]

6. Termination & Survival

  • If they terminate early without cause, they owe:
  • Remaining base for the term (or a severance formula), and
  • Performance fees on pipelines you already built for a defined tail period (e.g. 12 months).
  • Your exit / profit-interest rights survive termination as long as you met defined performance milestones.

[IF THEY FIRE YOU AFTER YOU HELP THEM WIN, YOU STILL PARTICIPATE WHEN THEY EVENTUALLY SELL]

How to use this

Your 12–18 month goal is to get 1–2 RIAs to sign a simplified version of this: - Clause 3 (base + performance) - One clean exit-upside clause from 4 - Basic data/termination language from 5–6

Save this and literally hand it to a lawyer as: "Turn this into a Growth Partnership Agreement for RIAs; protect me but keep the commercial intent."

Warren's notes (2026-11-27)

  • Two agreements, not one. Phase 1 = the pilot/client agreement everyone signs on day 0 (📝 task: promoter agreement + client disclosure + $2k store-credit deposit + current fee terms). Phase 2 = this Growth Partnership Agreement, offered to the top 1–2 after results. Don't bolt 4.x onto the pilot paper; it scares a first-time client. Instead, put one line in the pilot: "If the engagement continues past the pilot, the parties intend to negotiate a Growth Partnership Agreement including performance and exit participation." That's the hook without the fear.
  • Baseline on day 0 regardless. Clause 3.2/5 only works if trailing-12 fee revenue + household count are captured at install. Add to onboarding even for pilot clients: it's the case-study number too.
  • Numbers to pick before the lawyer: X (performance %: 10–20%), Y (profit interest: 5–10% of value above today's), Z (call-option size, e.g. up to 10% at 8–10x EBITDA), term (3 yrs), tail (12 months). Michael decides; Hormozi bot can pressure-test.
  • Compliance angle: an RIA granting equity/options to a marketing firm touches Form ADV disclosures and possibly the SEC Marketing Rule (compensation for endorsements/referrals must be disclosed). The lawyer needs to be a securities/RIA lawyer, not a general contracts one.
