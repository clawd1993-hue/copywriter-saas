You're not missing much. You just need a clean, boring machine for: Intro call (5 min) → VSL → Closing call (30–40 min). Use these as scaffolding, not poetry. Read them until you're bored.

Closing call script (30–40 minutes)

Goal: Get explicit yes to 30-day pilot and commitments: record webinar, record ads, let you run traffic.

A. Opening & frame (3–5 min)

"Hey [Name], good to see you again. How's your day so far?

Cool. I've got about 30–40 minutes blocked. Purpose here is pretty simple: - Make sure I understood your situation from what you told me and what you saw in the video, - Walk you through exactly how the Retirement Class funnel would plug into your firm, - If it looks like a fit on both sides, outline the 30-day pilot and we make a yes/no decision.

Sound good?"

Confirm they watched the VSL:

"Did you get a chance to watch that short video I sent over, the one walking through the funnel and the economics?"

If no:

"No worries. I'll give you the 2-minute version as we go so we can still decide if this makes sense."

B. Discovery / deepen pain (10–15 min)

"You gave me the quick overview before, but catch me up properly: - How are you currently getting new retiree households? - Roughly how many new households did you onboard last year? - What's a good new client for you in AUM terms? (500k? 1M? 2M+?) - When you think about the next 12–24 months, what would 'good' look like in terms of new households per month? - What have you already tried that hasn't worked the way you wanted? (Dinners, seminars, lead vendors, radio, etc.) - If nothing changed and you just kept doing what you're doing now for the next 2–3 years, what's the downside for you?"

Let them talk. You want their words as ammo for the close. Remember: more questions, fewer monologues. ($100M Money Models, p.49)

C. Transition: confirm desire & capacity (3–5 min)

"Got it. So, if I'm hearing you right, you'd like to be adding about [X] good new households a month in the [500k–1M / 1M+] range, and right now [current channel] is getting you [Y] with [pain]. Is that fair?"

Then:

"And if you had a system that could realistically add those extra [X] households a month, do you have the advisor capacity to actually take them on and service them properly?"

You need them to say yes here.

D. Walkthrough: how your system plugs in (7–10 min)

Screen share their personalized funnel while you talk:

"Okay, let me show you what this would look like in your world."

Walk: Registration page → TY + DM/AI flow → Class frame → Booking → calendar.

Language:

"From a high level, here's the sequence:

We invite 55–75 year-olds in [Area] with roughly 500k–1M+ saved into a branded 'Retirement Tax & Income Class' under your firm.

They register, we ask their asset range and timing. Anyone under your minimum can still watch, but we optimize everything around the 500k+ and especially 1M+ bucket.

Our AI assistant / setter has a real conversation, confirms fit, and offers a 45-minute 'Retirement Map Call' with you or your team. Qualified prospects book straight onto your calendar.

We watch the numbers weekly: cost per qualified reg, cost per booked meeting, show rates, and, over time, how many become clients."

Pause and check:

"Does that line up with how you like to bring people in, or is there anything there that feels off for [Firm]?"

Handle tweaks, but don't script-chase. Keep it simple.

E. Present the pilot offer (5–7 min)

Now go into "partnership" mode. Anchor to their economics. Average RIA around 1% AUM fee; a $1M client is ~$10k/year in fees. ($100M Offers, p.40)

"Cool. So here's what I'd suggest as a starting point.

We run this as a 30-day founding pilot for a tiny number of firms.

Our side: - We fully build and install the funnel on your domain - Customize the class script and ad scripts with your voice - Set up and manage the ad account - Install and monitor the AI assistant - Meet weekly to look at the pipeline and tweak

Your side: - Cover ad spend - Record the 45-minute class and 3 short ad videos in the first 7 days - Take the meetings the system books within 48 hours

For the pilot there's: No setup fee. No management retainer.

We only earn if you do: $1,000 for each new household we help you close with $500k+ in new assets, plus 10% of first-year advisory fees on those assets.

If at day 30 you've had at least 5 qualified meetings and you like the trajectory, we can talk about rolling into a 90-day engagement with a flat monthly plus the same success fees. If you hate it, we turn over everything we've built and you owe us nothing beyond any per-client success fees you already agreed to. It's a pure performance-aligned setup." ($100M Offers, p.135)

Then the close:

"Based on what you told me about wanting [X] more good households a month, does it make sense to claim one of these pilot slots and get this stood up for [Firm]?"

Shut up.

F. Objections (short handles)

"Compliance will never allow this."

"Totally get that. Step one after this call is introducing your CCO. Nothing goes live without their sign-off. The webinar is educational, no performance claims, no product pushes. All we're doing is organizing how people who need planning find you."

"We don't really do webinars."

"Think of this as your steak-dinner talk without the steak. Same education, same people, less overhead, more measurable."

"Why free? What's the catch?"

"The 'catch' is I want three killer case studies. I'm betting my time and team that we can be your best source of new AUM. If I'm wrong, you walk with a built system and some extra conversations. If I'm right, we both make a lot of money and we keep working together."

"Let me think about it."

"Totally fine. Just so I understand, is it more about fit, economics, or compliance? … [address real thing] Given we're only doing this with three firms, what would you need to see or know to feel comfortable saying yes or no in the next 48 hours?"

Aim to leave with a clear yes/no or a scheduled decision call.

Note (Warren): Hormozi says "founding pilot" and "5 qualified meetings." Our locked wording is "30-day pilot" (no "founding") and 10 meetings. Reference copy left as he wrote it.
