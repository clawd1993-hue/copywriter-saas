STAGE 4 · SETTER DM SCRIPT

Runs in GHL (SMS) or ManyChat (Messenger). Setter name = a real first name, from [Firm]. Tone: warm, short, texts like a person. One question per message. Never two.

Michael's order: link first → location → quals → watch-gate → book. (Delta from Hormozi's draft, which held the link until after quals and pushed pre-class booking. Michael's call. Link-first = lower friction; watch-gate = higher show + better call quality.)

4.1 — Link + location check ⚡ draft

Hey [First], it's [Setter] from [Firm]. Thanks for grabbing a seat for the Retirement Tax & Income Class.

Here's your private link 👉 [WEBINAR LINK]

Real quick, where are you based? Just want to make sure the class applies to you.

  • In [State] → 4.2
  • Out of state → "Got it. The class is built around [State] rules so some of it won't apply, but you're welcome to watch." Tag OUT-OF-STATE. No call offer. Stop.
  • No reply 2h → "Still there, [First]? Just need your city so I can confirm your spot." · 24h → move on to reminders, no quals.

4.2 — Bridge to quals ⚡ draft

Awesome, [City]'s right in our wheelhouse.

A couple quick questions so I can pass them to the team and they can get started on your personalized Retirement Map before the class. Takes 30 seconds. Cool?

(Yes / 👍 / any reply → 4.3. Silence → send 4.3 anyway after 1h.)

4.3 — Qual 1: assets (Hormozi wording)

First one. About how much have you saved for retirement so far, not counting your home? a) Under $250,000 b) $250,000–$500,000 c) $500,000–$1,000,000 d) $1,000,000–$2,000,000 e) $2,000,000+

(Buttons on Messenger, free-type on SMS. Re-tag if it differs from the form answer. c–e → QUALIFIED.)

4.4 — Qual 2: timeline ⚡ draft

Got it. And roughly when are you planning to retire, or are you already there?

(Any answer accepted. Within 10 yrs or retired = ideal. Logs to contact.)

4.5 — Qual 3: motivation (Hormozi wording)

Last one. What made you decide to register today? a) I'm worried about taxes in retirement b) I'm not sure if I'll have enough / might run out c) I'm retiring in the next few years and want a second opinion d) Something else

Mirror:

Got it – a lot of people we talk to feel the same way about "[their answer]" before they see the numbers.

4.6 — Confirm + set expectation ⚡ draft

Perfect, that's everything. I've passed it to the team so your Retirement Map's ready to go after the class.

Your link again 👉 [WEBINAR LINK] Watch whenever suits you, phone, tablet or computer, it's about 45 minutes. I'll check in tomorrow to see if you got to it.

(If the client runs it LIVE instead of on-demand: "Your link for [Day] at [Time] [TZ] 👉 [WEBINAR LINK]. I'll ping you the day before and an hour before.")

After you've watched, I'll send you a link to grab a free 1:1 Retirement Map Call with [Advisor First] where he plugs your actual numbers into the 5-step map. Sound good?

(Stop here. Do NOT send the calendar link yet. That's the watch-gate.)

Exception, QUALIFIED-HI only (Hormozi's early-book, keep as an option): ⚡ draft

One more thing while you're here. Spots for the Retirement Map Call usually fill up right after the class, so some people grab a time in advance and keep it only if the class is useful. Want me to send you the calendar link now? → Yes: [CALENDAR LINK] "Pick a time after the class. If it's not for you, cancel, no hard feelings." → No: "Totally fine. Watch the class, I'll send it after."

4.7 — Reminders

On-demand (default): 24h after link: "Hey [First], did you get a chance to start the class yet? Link's here if not: [LINK]. About 45 minutes, worth a quiet hour." · 72h: one more, then watch-gate.

Live (if the client schedules sessions), Hormozi verbatim: 24h before:

Hey [First], quick reminder: your Retirement Tax & Income Class is tomorrow at [Time] [TZ]. Here's your link again: [LINK]. Try to watch with your spouse/partner if you can – the couples who attend together get the most out of it.

1h before:

Hey [First], we're starting in about an hour. Your link: [LINK]. See you there.

4.8 — Watch-gate ⚡ draft

Fires 2h after the class ends (or next morning if class is evening).

Hey [First], did you get a chance to watch the class?

  • Yes → 4.9
  • Part of it → "No worries, the Retirement Map part is the back half, slides 7 onward. Here's the link to finish it: [LINK]. Ping me when you're through and I'll send you [Advisor First]'s calendar." · re-check 24h.
  • No → "All good, it's recorded. Here's the link: [LINK]. It's about 45 minutes. Watch it when you've got a quiet hour and let me know, I'll hold a Retirement Map Call slot for you." · re-check 24h, then 72h, then drop to the post-class sequence.
  • Silence → 24h: "Still want me to hold a Retirement Map Call slot for you, [First]?" · 72h: last touch, post-class sequence.

4.9 — Book ⚡ draft

Great. Quick one: what stood out most?

(Let them answer. Mirror one line. Then:)

Makes sense. That's exactly what the Retirement Map Call is for. [Advisor First] takes your numbers and walks through the 5 steps for your situation, about 30 minutes, no cost, no pitch.

Here's his calendar 👉 [CALENDAR LINK] Grab whatever's easiest this week or next.

Booked → 4.10. Not booked in 24h → "Did the times not work? I can get you something else." 48h → "Last nudge from me, [First]. If you want your Retirement Map done, grab a slot here: [CALENDAR LINK]. Otherwise no worries, you've got the class."

4.10 — Booking confirmation + show-up ⚡ draft

You're booked for [Day] at [Time] with [Advisor First]. Calendar invite's on its way.

Two things to have handy: a rough list of your accounts and balances, and your Social Security estimate if you've got it. Makes the map a lot more useful.

See you then.

24h before call: "Reminder, your Retirement Map Call with [Advisor First] is tomorrow at [Time]. Still good?" 1h before: "Your call's in an hour. [Zoom/phone details]." No-show: within 15 min: "Hey [First], [Advisor First]'s on the line, everything ok?" · 2h: "No worries, life happens. Grab a new time here: [CALENDAR LINK]." · 24h: one more. Then drop to nurture.

4.11 — Post-class, didn't book (Hormozi, verbatim)

Hey [First], this is [Setter] from [Firm]. Saw you caught our Retirement Tax & Income Class – thanks for spending part of your day with us. If you'd like us to plug your numbers into the 5-step Retirement Map we walked through, here's the link to grab a free 1:1 call: [CALENDAR LINK]. We only open a handful of spots each month, so if you want one, grab it while they're open.

Setter FAQ answers ⚡ draft (the questions that actually come in)

They ask Setter says
"Is this a sales pitch?" "No. The class is education only, nothing's sold. The Retirement Map Call is optional and free. If you want [Advisor First]'s help after, that's a separate conversation you'd have with him."
"Who is [Advisor]?" "[Advisor First] is a [CFP® / fiduciary] at [Firm] here in [City]. He's been doing retirement income planning for [State] families for [X] years. His bio's on the registration page."
"Is it live or recorded?" "It's a scheduled online class. You join at [Time] from the link. If you can't make it, let me know and I'll get you the replay."
"Can my spouse join?" "Please do. Same link, one screen. The couples who watch together get the most out of it."
"How did you get my number?" "You entered it when you registered for the class at [page]. If that wasn't you, reply STOP and I'll remove you."
"I'm not in [State]." "The class is built around [State] rules so some won't apply, but you're welcome to watch." No call offer.
"Is this free?" "Yes. The class and the Retirement Map Call are both free. No cost, no obligation."
Anything about returns, products, specific accounts "That's exactly the kind of thing [Advisor First] goes through on the Retirement Map Call. I'm the scheduling side, I don't give advice."

Hard rules for the setter

  • Never discuss performance, returns, products, or specific securities. Redirect to the call.
  • Never promise outcomes.
  • Promoter disclosure line available on request and included in the first message's footer link.
  • STOP = stop. Immediately.
  • One question per message. Short. Like a person.

Advisor's 1:1. 30 min. He runs the 5 steps on their numbers, shows them their own map, invites them to become a client. This is his sales call, not ours. We get him there with a pre-framed, pre-qualified, already-educated household.
