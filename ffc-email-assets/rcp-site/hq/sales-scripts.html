4 · CALL 1 — THE INTRO CALL (booked as 15, runs ~5)

Phone or Zoom audio. No slides, no screenshare. Michael's script (voice note 2026-11-11). One goal: qualify, disclose the comp structure, book the 20-min.

Open

"How's it going, [First]? Congrats on the [AUM]M under management, by the way. Are you guys actively looking to scale right now?"

→ Yes.

Disclosure (the filter)

"Cool. Full disclosure, like I said, we manage pipelines of high-net-worth individuals. We work with a ton of proprietary data, large data sets of HNWIs, so we can put you in front of the right people and get you qualified meetings. And we install a sales team on your side that pre-qualifies every single one before they reach you. Just to be transparent about our comp structure: we only get paid when somebody we send you closes with an RIA, with assets of $500k plus. So we just want to make sure we're working with partners who are actively looking to grow, who are motivated to close deals and actually work the leads we send your way. Does that sound like a fit?"

→ Yes.

Book

"Awesome. Let's schedule a call where I'm at my laptop and can actually walk you through it on screen. Does [day] at [time] or [day] at [time] work?"

Lock the slot on the call. Then send the confirmation email (§5) with the VSL and their page. Pitch happens on call 2, after they've watched the VSL.

Pre-call prep (2 min): AUM from ria-top50.csv, their page open, the "congrats" number right.

Deflections ⚡ draft - "Just send me info." → "Totally. I'll send a short video that shows the whole thing, plus the page we built for [Firm]. Let's put 20 minutes on the calendar after you've watched it. If it's not a fit you'll know in ten." - "What does it cost?" → "Nothing up front for the pilot firms. No setup, no retainer. We only get paid when a household closes. The exact structure is the last five minutes of the next call, I'd rather you see the machine first." - "Is this a webinar thing?" → "It's your steak-dinner talk without the steak. Same retiree, same content, from their living room, and it runs every week without you leaving the office." - "I need to check with compliance." → "Good. They'll be looped in before anything goes live. Nothing I show you on the next call is public. Let's get it on the calendar and you bring your CCO's hard lines to it." - "I'll think about it." → "Sure. What specifically, fit or timing?" Handle, then re-offer two slots.

"Proprietary data" on call 1 (Michael 2026-11-12): keep it at the headline, "large data sets of HNWIs" + "a sales team that pre-qualifies everyone." Do NOT explain what the data is. The reveal is saved for call 2 so there's something new to get excited about.

On this call: one line only: "we only get paid when you get paid." Never on this call: fee numbers, the $2k deposit, guarantee details, ad-spend numbers, the webinar script. All of that lives on call 2.

6 · CALL 2 — THE 20-MINUTE CLOSE

Zoom, screenshare. Hormozi's script, verbatim, with Michael's additions in the money section. Have open: the slide deck, their LP, their TY page, the setter flow, the one-pager.

Michael's notes for this call (2026-11-11): - Slide deck. Walk the pitch off a deck: expected metrics (cost per registrant, show rate, qualified rate, booked meetings per $1k spend) + one case study. Numbers come from Hormozi's doc (Michael has it, not yet in the workspace). Deck = open item below. - "Proprietary data" reveal slide (Michael 2026-11-12), before the walkthrough. Call 1 said "large data sets of HNWIs." Here you open the box:

"On the first call I mentioned proprietary data. Here's what that actually is. We run a Meta pixel that's been trained on thousands of high-net-worth retirees who've registered, qualified and booked through funnels like this one. So when we launch yours, we're not starting from zero and hoping Facebook figures out who's wealthy. The algorithm already knows what a $750k household looks like, and it goes and finds more of them in [City]. That's why our cost per qualified meeting is where it is, and why your first weeks aren't a blind test." Same asset, two framings: call 1 = "proprietary data + a sales team," call 2 = "a pixel trained on thousands of HNWIs." The reframe is on purpose, it keeps the excitement climbing between calls. (Compliance: this is a marketing-infrastructure claim, not a performance claim. Don't attach numbers to it beyond the case study.) - Case study slide (Hormozi, 2026-11-12, verbatim numbers): estate-planning + AUM shop. Front door: Meta ads → webinar reg page → thank-you → live webinar → call → sell a $4k trust + feed AUM. Modelled:

Stage Number
Ad spend ~$25k
Leads ~1,250 at ~$20 each
Live attendees ~300
Calls booked ~40
Calls showed ~32
Sales ($4k service) ~12 = ~$48k front-end + AUM backend

Say it like this: "Cold traffic, older demo, financial topic, on Meta, at scale. The constraint was never 'Facebook doesn't work.' It was optimising for wealthy leads instead of cheap opt-ins." (Anonymised: "a shop we helped." No firm name, no returns, no AUM figures = Marketing-Rule safe.) ⚠️ Warren's math on this slide: 40 calls on $25k = 1.6 booked calls per $1k. At the $100/day floor ($3k/30 days) that's ~5 booked, ~4 showed. The 10-meeting guarantee needs ~$6.5k spend (~$215/day) on these ratios. See Open items. - Disclaimer slide, right after the case study (Michael's words, say it out loud):

"Disclaimer: these numbers only work when you do. We're very good at what we do, and we'll be an extension of your right hand: working with your team on the leads, listening to your calls, giving feedback. We'll fight tooth and nail to help you close as many clients and add as much AUM as possible, because again, we only get paid when you do."

Why it's there: it turns the case study into a condition, not a promise (compliance-safe), and it pre-frames the "your side" list in the pitch. Deck order: metrics → case study → disclaimer → pitch. - $2k deposit (Michael 2026-11-12). Say it after "no setup fee, no retainer," before the close.

Short form (the beats, say it in your own words):

$2k deposit, two reasons. Holds your spot because one RIA per area and we're turning the others down. Aligns incentives so we can get the recording done asap and because the appointments need working the minute they land, so we both have skin in the game. "And it comes with our win-your-money-back guarantee: land your first five appointments and the $2,000 comes back to you. If you do your part, this costs you nothing." Then the close.

Full script:

"One more thing, and I want to be upfront about it. There's a $2,000 deposit to get started. Two reasons.

First, it holds your spot. We only work with one RIA per area, so once you're in, [City] is yours and we're turning down the other firms we're talking to around you.

Second, it lines up our incentives. We need the class recorded fast so we can get ads live, and the qualified, ready-to-plan appointments need to be worked the minute they land. The $2k means we both have skin in the game: you're motivated to get the recording done and take the meetings, we're motivated to fill your calendar as soon as possible. That's how we make sure the ads are profitable from day one and you want to keep working with us. Because remember, we only make money when you close.

And it comes with our win-your-money-back guarantee: land your first five appointments and the $2,000 comes back to you. So the way this is set up, if you do your part, this costs you nothing." Then straight into the close: "Given that, is there any reason you wouldn't want us to get this stood up for you?"

Rules for this script: - "Win your money back" / "comes back to you." Never "refund," "refundable," "money back guarantee." - Do not explain the mechanism on the call. The $2k comes back as credit against the $5k/mo engagement, written in the agreement. If they ask "how do I get it back": "It's applied back to your account once you've hit five appointments, it's in the agreement, I'll send it over right after this." Then move on. - "First five appointments" = five qualified meetings booked through the funnel, not five closed households. Keep the bar low enough that it's clearly winnable; the point is the commitment, not the $2k. - The spot-holding line only works because the metro rule is real at install. Don't say it to two firms in the same metro on the same day. - ⚠️ Warren's flag, once, then it's your call: "win your money back" + credit-only is the kind of line a CCO or an angry client screenshots. If one firm ever walks after five appointments and asks for cash, be ready to just pay it. $2k is cheap insurance against a reputation hit in a 4,000-firm market.

  • Call 1 never goes past "we only get paid when you get paid." All numbers, the deposit, the guarantee: this call only.

Frame (1 min)

"Appreciate you hopping on, [First]. Goal for the next 20 minutes is simple: – Make sure what we built actually fits your practice, – Show you how the funnel works, – If it looks like a fit, explain the 30-day pilot for 3 firms and you can say yes or no. Sound fair?"

Discovery (5 min)

"How are you currently getting new retiree households?" "What's a good new client for you in AUM terms?" "If this worked, how many new households a month could you realistically absorb?" "Any hard lines from compliance we need to know before we touch anything?"

⚡ draft Write their answers down. You'll quote them back in the close ("you said a good client is $800k…").

Walkthrough (5 min)

Your role: record the webinar once, record 2–3 short ad clips, then just take qualified meetings."

Pitch (4 min)

"We're looking for 3 RIAs to run this as a 30-day pilot.

What we do: – Install this funnel on your domain – Customize the webinar script and ads for you – Run and manage the ad account – Install and monitor the AI setter – Meet weekly to review pipeline.

Your side: – You cover ad spend – You record the webinar and ad clips within 7 days – You or your team take booked meetings within 48 hours.

For the pilot there's no setup fee, no retainer. We only earn if you do: $1,000 per new household at $500k+ plus 10% of first-year advisory fees on assets originated from this funnel. After 30 days we both look at the math and decide if we keep going on a paid basis."

Money positioning (fold into the pitch, Hormozi verbatim)

"We run this as a performance-partnership. You keep control of your brand, domain, and client relationships. You cover media; we cover build and brainpower.

For the first 30 days: – No setup fee – No management retainer – You keep the funnel even if we never work together again.

Our upside is tied to yours: – $1,000 for each new household at $500k+ – Plus 10% of the first-year advisory fees on assets that came from this funnel.

If, at day 30, you like the numbers, we roll into a 90-day engagement at $5k/month plus the same performance structure. If you don't, we turn everything over to your team and part friends."

"The ongoing is 5k/month plus the same success fees; we only offer that after we've proven it makes sense for you."

Guarantee (exact words):

The close

"Given what you've told me, is there any reason you wouldn't want us to get this stood up for you as one of the 3 pilots?"

Then shut up.

Objections (Hormozi, verbatim)

Compliance:

"Perfect, your compliance officer becomes our best friend. Everything runs through them before it goes live. The webinar is educational only: no performance, no guarantees, no product pushes. Step one after this call is looping them in and letting them red-pen everything."

"We don't do webinars":

"Totally. Think of this as your steak dinner talk without the steak. Same content, same retiree, just from their living room. You already know education-based events work; this just makes it evergreen."

"Who are you / no track record":

"Fair. This is why I'm only offering this structure to 3 firms. You get a fully installed client machine with our time at risk. Worst case, you keep the funnel and data and never pay us a dollar. Best case, you get a predictable source of new households and we become a long-term partner."

"What's the catch if it's free":

"The 'catch' is I want wins and case studies. I'm betting my time and team on being able to deliver you new AUM. If we don't, you walk with a built asset and some extra exposure; if we do, we both make money and we keep working together."

"We already have marketing / we use FMG or Snappy Kraken": ⚡ draft

"Good, keep it, it's your newsletter. What it can't do is go find a $750k household in [City], qualify them on assets and timeline, and put them on your calendar. That's what we do, and we get paid only when it works."

(Snappy Kraken = $199–899/mo software: templates, content library, drips, forms. No ads, no qualification, no booking. FMG = same category.)

"We tried SmartAsset / a lead vendor": ⚡ draft

"Right, and you were one of three advisors on every lead. These are your leads, on your page, nobody else sees them."

"We already use White Glove / do seminars": ⚡ draft

"They fill a room. We fill your calendar with people who already told us what they've got."

"We're with Zoe" (if it comes up): ⚡ draft

"Zoe owns that client, you're the inventory. Here the page is yours, the list is yours, the relationship is yours from the first text."

"I'll think about it":

"Totally fine. Just so I know, what specifically do you want to think about? Fit, compliance, or the economics? … [handle the real concern] Also, I only have 3 pilot slots. I can hold one with your name on it for 48 hours. Do you want me to do that while you talk with [partner/compliance]?"

⚡ draft Metro pressure (Michael's rule, 2026-11-11). We contact every firm in a metro. Overlap is leverage, not a problem. Use it on "I'll think about it," on the 48h hold, and on any stall:

"One thing you should know: I'm also talking with [Other Firm] down the road. One firm per metro gets this, so if you want [City], I need to know by [date]. If you pass, no hard feelings, I'll go with them."

(Only name a real competitor you're actually in touch with. Never bluff a name; "another firm in [City]" is fine if you don't want to say who.)

⚡ draft "How much ad spend?" (Michael's number, 2026-11-11: pitch $100/day, then explain more is better)

"A hundred dollars a day. That's the floor, about three thousand for the pilot, in your own ad account so you see every dollar. Honestly, more is better. The first two weeks are the pixel learning who a qualified retiree in [City] looks like, and the more it sees, the faster it locks on. Firms that run a hundred and fifty or two hundred a day get there sooner and get more meetings out of the same thirty days. But a hundred is where we start, and it's less than one dinner seminar."

(Anchor is $100/day. Don't go below it. If they volunteer more, take it. Say the "more is better" line every time, so the $100 reads as a floor, not a budget.)

⚡ draft "Can you guarantee AUM?"

"No, and anyone who does is lying to you or breaking a rule. What I guarantee is qualified meetings on your calendar. Closing them is your craft, and you're good at it or you wouldn't be at [$AUM]."

⚡ draft "10% of fees on top of $1k is a lot."

"It's $1,000 plus roughly $1,000 on a $1M household in year one. Two grand for a client worth six figures to you over the relationship, and zero if we don't deliver. If after the pilot you'd rather restructure, we'll have real numbers to do that with."
