Outbound → 5-min intro → 20-min close. One doc, in order. v1.2 · 2026-11-11 · Hormozi copy = verbatim (law), except "founding" → "pilot" everywhere per Michael. Warren drafts marked ⚡ draft — Michael sharpens.

The motion: every message drives to the 5-min call. The 5-min call drives to the 20-min call. Never pitch on the 5-min. Close on the 20-min.

Swap tokens: [First] [Firm] [City] [Area] [Niche angle] [Link] = https://hnwacquisition.com/for/<slug>/ [5-min booking link] [Zoom link]

1 · MESSAGE 1 — THE HOOK (day 0)

Email, no subject (or "quick q"). Same words on LinkedIn DM. Michael's. Mandatory. Never skip. v1.2, 2026-11-11.

Hook (first touch):

Are you taking on more clients?

Bridge (the second they reply yes / depends / who's asking):

Awesome. We work primarily with individuals with assets valued between 750k–1m actively searching for RIAs in their local area, and we're looking for someone in the area that we can send all our qualified leads to.

Is that something that you'd be interested in?

Then: on "yes" / "tell me more" → Message 2 (the build + link), same thread.

Reply handling ⚡ draft

They say You send
"Yes" / "Depends" / "Who's asking?" The bridge above. Then Message 2 on the next yes.
"No, we're full" "Got it. Out of curiosity, is that a capacity thing or a lead-quality thing? Asking because what we built for [Firm] is specifically $750k–1M households, not volume." Then the bridge if they engage.
"Not interested" "Understood, I'll close your file." Stop.
Nothing after 2 days Message 2 as a new email.

2 · MESSAGE 2 — THE BUILD (day 0–2)

Subject: Built this for [Firm] — Hormozi body, verbatim. Top + bottom "free" lines added by Michael (2026-11-11), drafted ⚡ draft.

[First],

Short version: we already built something for [Firm], and it's yours free. Details below.

I work with independent RIAs in the $100–250M range who want more local retirees with $1M+ to manage.

We took your ADV and website and pre-built a "Retirement Tax & Income Class" webinar funnel specifically for [Firm] – your name, headshot, [City], and retiree positioning are already wired in.

It's designed to consistently create booked meetings with $1M+ households in [Area], off a 45-minute educational class you record once.

If you're curious, here's the private preview: [Link]. No opt-in.

Here's the part that matters: if it fits, we want to install the whole thing for you. The funnel on your domain, plus a 24/7 qualification specialist that contacts every lead, qualifies them on assets, and books the serious ones straight onto your calendar. Completely free. No setup fee, no retainer. We're doing this for 3 firms.

Worth a quick 5-minute call this week so I can walk you through what it does? Reply with a time, or grab one here: [5-min booking link]

Why the two lines ⚡ draft: the top line reframes the email as a gift before they decide whether to keep reading. The bottom line is the actual offer, stated once, hard, right before the ask. "3 firms" gives the free a reason. Hormozi's body in between is untouched.

If they reply with a question instead of a time ⚡ draft — answer in one line, re-ask for the 5 min. Don't explain the model in email.

"Short version: you record a class once, we run it to [City] retirees with $500k+, our team qualifies every registrant and books the real ones on your calendar. Five minutes on the phone and you'll know if it's for you. [5-min booking link]"

4 · CALL 1 — THE 5-MINUTE INTRO ⚡ draft

Phone or Zoom audio. No slides, no screenshare. One goal: book the 20-min.

Open (30s)

"[First], Michael. Appreciate you picking up. I'll keep this to five minutes. Did you get a chance to open the page with your face on it?"

  • Saw it → "What went through your head when you saw it?" (let them talk, 30s, don't interrupt)
  • Didn't → "No problem, I'll resend after this. Two-second version: it's a retirement tax and income class under your brand, built to put $500k+ local households on your calendar."

Qualify (90s) — two questions only

"Are you actively taking on new households right now?" "What's a good new client for you, roughly, in assets?"

Listen for: taking clients (yes/no), AUM floor, whether they do seminars/dinners today. Don't fix anything. Don't pitch.

Explain (60s)

"Here's what we do. You record a 45-minute class once. We run ads to retirees in [City] with $500k+ saved, our team qualifies and follows up with every registrant, and the serious ones get booked straight onto your calendar. You take the meeting. We're running a 30-day pilot with three firms, no setup fee and no retainer; we only earn when you sign a household."

Book (60s)

"I don't want to do this justice in five minutes. Let's do 20 on Zoom where I share my screen and walk you through your actual funnel end to end, and you can decide if it's a fit. Does [day] at [time] or [day] at [time] work?"

Lock the slot on the call. Send the invite + confirmation email before you hang up or within 5 minutes.

Deflections - "Just send me info." → "Totally. The page is the info; there's nothing else to read. The 20 minutes is me showing you how it plugs into your calendar and compliance. If it's not a fit you'll know in ten." - "What does it cost?" → "For the three pilot firms, nothing up front. No setup, no retainer. We only earn when you sign a household. The exact structure is the last five minutes of the 20-min call, I'd rather you see the machine first." - "Is this a webinar thing?" → "It's your steak-dinner talk without the steak. Same retiree, same content, from their living room, and it runs every week without you leaving the office." - "I need to check with compliance." → "Good. They'll be looped in before anything goes live. Nothing I show you on the 20-min is public. Let's get the call on the calendar and you bring your CCO's hard lines to it." - "I'll think about it." → "Sure. What specifically, fit or timing?" Handle, then re-offer two slots.

Never on this call: fee numbers, guarantee details, ad-spend numbers, the webinar script. All of that lives on call 2.

6 · CALL 2 — THE 20-MINUTE CLOSE

Zoom, screenshare. Hormozi's script, verbatim, with Michael's additions in the money section. Have open: their LP, their TY page, the setter flow, the one-pager.

Frame (1 min)

"Appreciate you hopping on, [First]. Goal for the next 20 minutes is simple: – Make sure what we built actually fits your practice, – Show you how the funnel works, – If it looks like a fit, explain the 30-day pilot for 3 firms and you can say yes or no. Sound fair?"

Discovery (5 min)

"How are you currently getting new retiree households?" "What's a good new client for you in AUM terms?" "If this worked, how many new households a month could you realistically absorb?" "Any hard lines from compliance we need to know before we touch anything?"

⚡ draft Write their answers down. You'll quote them back in the close ("you said a good client is $800k…").

Walkthrough (5 min)

Share screen: their LP → TY page → setter flow.

"This is the 'Retirement Tax & Income Class' funnel we mocked up for you. – Ads / traffic go here. – Retirees register for the class. – After they watch, our 24/7 AI assistant filters for $500k–$1M+ and books straight onto your calendar. Your role: record the webinar once, record 2–3 short ad clips, then just take qualified meetings."

Pitch (4 min)

"We're looking for 3 RIAs to run this as a 30-day pilot.

What we do: – Install this funnel on your domain – Customize the webinar script and ads for you – Run and manage the ad account – Install and monitor the AI setter – Meet weekly to review pipeline.

Your side: – You cover ad spend – You record the webinar and ad clips within 7 days – You or your team take booked meetings within 48 hours.

For the pilot there's no setup fee, no retainer. We only earn if you do: $1,000 per new household at $500k+ plus 10% of first-year advisory fees on assets originated from this funnel. After 30 days we both look at the math and decide if we keep going on a paid basis."

Money positioning (fold into the pitch, Hormozi verbatim)

"We run this as a performance-partnership. You keep control of your brand, domain, and client relationships. You cover media; we cover build and brainpower.

For the first 30 days: – No setup fee – No management retainer – You keep the funnel even if we never work together again.

Our upside is tied to yours: – $1,000 for each new household at $500k+ – Plus 10% of the first-year advisory fees on assets that came from this funnel.

If, at day 30, you like the numbers, we roll into a 90-day engagement at $5k/month plus the same performance structure. If you don't, we turn everything over to your team and part friends."

"The ongoing is 5k/month plus the same success fees; we only offer that after we've proven it makes sense for you."

⚡ draft Say these two things before they sign (from Clawd's notes, non-negotiable): 1. Ugly first weeks. "The first two to four weeks of ads will look rough while the pixel learns what a qualified retiree looks like. That's normal. Here's the budget and the timeline. Hold the line and don't read the daily numbers." 2. The LTV math. "One $1M household is roughly $10k a year to you, $100k-plus over the relationship. Six households pays a full year of the ongoing. That's the bar we're measuring against."

Guarantee (exact words):

"If by day 30 you haven't had at least 5 qualified meetings ($500k+, fit your niche), we'll either work an additional 30 days at no management fee or help your team take the funnel in-house and end the pilot with no performance fees owed."

The close

"Given what you've told me, is there any reason you wouldn't want us to get this stood up for you as one of the 3 pilots?"

Then shut up.

Objections (Hormozi, verbatim)

Compliance:

"Perfect, your compliance officer becomes our best friend. Everything runs through them before it goes live. The webinar is educational only: no performance, no guarantees, no product pushes. Step one after this call is looping them in and letting them red-pen everything."

"We don't do webinars":

"Totally. Think of this as your steak dinner talk without the steak. Same content, same retiree, just from their living room. You already know education-based events work; this just makes it evergreen."

"Who are you / no track record":

"Fair. This is why I'm only offering this structure to 3 firms. You get a fully installed client machine with our time at risk. Worst case, you keep the funnel and data and never pay us a dollar. Best case, you get a predictable source of new households and we become a long-term partner."

"What's the catch if it's free":

"The 'catch' is I want wins and case studies. I'm betting my time and team on being able to deliver you new AUM. If we don't, you walk with a built asset and some extra exposure; if we do, we both make money and we keep working together."

"I'll think about it":

"Totally fine. Just so I know, what specifically do you want to think about? Fit, compliance, or the economics? … [handle the real concern] Also, I only have 3 pilot slots. I can hold one with your name on it for 48 hours. Do you want me to do that while you talk with [partner/compliance]?"

⚡ draft Metro pressure (Michael's rule, 2026-11-11). We contact every firm in a metro. Overlap is leverage, not a problem. Use it on "I'll think about it," on the 48h hold, and on any stall:

"One thing you should know: I'm also talking with [Other Firm] down the road. One firm per metro gets this, so if you want [City], I need to know by [date]. If you pass, no hard feelings, I'll go with them."

(Only name a real competitor you're actually in touch with. Never bluff a name; "another firm in [City]" is fine if you don't want to say who.)

⚡ draft "How much ad spend?" (Michael's number, 2026-11-11: pitch $100/day, then explain more is better)

"A hundred dollars a day. That's the floor, about three thousand for the pilot, in your own ad account so you see every dollar. Honestly, more is better. The first two weeks are the pixel learning who a qualified retiree in [City] looks like, and the more it sees, the faster it locks on. Firms that run a hundred and fifty or two hundred a day get there sooner and get more meetings out of the same thirty days. But a hundred is where we start, and it's less than one dinner seminar."

(Anchor is $100/day. Don't go below it. If they volunteer more, take it. Say the "more is better" line every time, so the $100 reads as a floor, not a budget.)

⚡ draft "Can you guarantee AUM?"

"No, and anyone who does is lying to you or breaking a rule. What I guarantee is qualified meetings on your calendar. Closing them is your craft, and you're good at it or you wouldn't be at [$AUM]."

⚡ draft "10% of fees on top of $1k is a lot."

"It's $1,000 plus roughly $1,000 on a $1M household in year one. Two grand for a client worth six figures to you over the relationship, and zero if we don't deliver. If after the pilot you'd rather restructure, we'll have real numbers to do that with."

If YES — on the call, before you hang up

  1. "Great. Three things happen now." Say them: - Promoter agreement + client disclosure go to your CCO today with a "please redline" note. - Recording checklist (45-min class + 3 ad clips) comes to you today. Seven-day clock. - We connect your calendar and ad account this week.
  2. Get the CCO's name + email on the call.
  3. Get their calendar tool + CRM on the call.
  4. Confirm who takes booked meetings and the 48-hour rule.
  5. Send the one-pager + agreement within the hour.

If "let me think" — hold the slot

Send same day: "Holding the [City] slot for [Firm] until [date, 48h]. I'm in conversation with one other firm in your area, so I can't hold it past that. Here's the one-pager and your page. Shout with questions." Follow up at 24h and 48h. At 48h: release the slot in writing. Don't beg.
