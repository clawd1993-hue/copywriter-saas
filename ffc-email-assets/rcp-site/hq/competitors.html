Competitors · what they offer · what we do differently (2026-11-24)

1 · Software / content (sell tools, not clients)

  • Snappy Kraken (snappykraken.com/pricing): $199 / $299 / $899 per month on annual terms + $199–499 setup. AI email builder, newsletters, birthday/market-update drips, "lead generation" content, Convos texting on upper tiers, website add-on $99–199/mo (+$499–2,499 setup), broker-dealer/compliance review. Does not run ads, does not qualify anyone, does not book anything. The advisor still has to find the prospect.
  • FMG Suite (fmgsuite.com): same category, older/bigger. Compliant content library, websites, email, social, compliant texting; "Do It For Me" tier. Same gap: content and a website, no acquisition.

2 · Lead vendors (sell the prospect, not the system)

  • SmartAsset AMP (get.smartasset.com): ~$25k/yr subscription. Consumer fills a quiz on SmartAsset, gets matched, concierge validates and live-connects. Same lead goes to up to 3 advisors at once; speed-to-dial decides who wins. Their own math assumes a 3–4% close rate.
  • Zoe Financial (zoefin.com): no per-lead fee; the advisor pays Zoe a share of the client's revenue for 5+ years (or a big lump sum); CFP/CFA + 5-yr minimum to join; Zoe keeps the relationship and the brand. SEC censured + $450k penalty in 2026 for conflict-disclosure failures (RIABiz, 2026-10-28).
  • Datalign, WiserAdvisor, Planswell ($450+/mo, one advisor per lead, comes with a plan): same model, different price points.
  • Retail appointment sellers: Revenx ($90–150 per pre-set appointment + $2k onboarding; pool is state/university/federal employees; annuity/IUL slant; comes with closing training), Retirement Prospects ($32 annuity lead via Facebook), Skyline Social (leads $5–50, self-booked appointments ~$200, advisor funds $1–3k ad spend).

3 · Seminar fillers (fill a room, you do the rest)

  • AcquireUp (owns White Glove, LeadJig, TouchFuse, Acquire Direct): dinner seminars + webinars; "Performance-Based Campaigns: pay only for households that attend"; direct mail + Facebook + venues; Express Digital low-cost webinar fills. No asset qualification, no booking, no follow-up; the advisor works the room and the no-shows.
  • The Anywhere Advisor, Azella: smaller versions of the same.

4 · Pay-for-performance agencies (closest to us, all weak)

  • Advanced Agent Marketing (annuity-focused), Revenx (above), OJay Media, Clientell. Mostly pay-per-appointment or retainer + ads; none install a system the advisor owns; none qualify on assets before booking; none price on closed households.

What we do differently (the list Michael asked for)

  • We qualify every lead for you: assets ($500k+), timeline, state, before a human at your firm touches it. Vendors send names; seminar firms send attendees; we send people who already told us what they have.
  • We book your calendar: the AI setter texts, answers questions, and puts the qualified household on your calendar. Nobody else ends at a booked meeting.
  • Exclusive, on your page, under your name: no 3-advisor lead splits (SmartAsset), no vendor-owned relationship (Zoe). The funnel, the list, the data are yours.
  • Education-first, not dinner-first: a 45-min class your retiree watches from the couch, evergreen, not a steak dinner you have to show up for every month.
  • We run the ads in your ad account, with your money, visible to you. Content tools don't; lead vendors keep the traffic.
  • Paid on results: no setup, no retainer, a fee per closed household with $500k+ AUM. Nobody else gets paid only when you sign the client.
  • One firm per metro: your competitor down the road can't buy the same system from us.
  • Installed, not rented: GHL sub-account, workflows, scripts, funnel on your domain; if we part ways you keep the machine.
  • Compliance baked in: SEC Marketing Rule language in the templates, every text and call logged and exportable for your CCO.

Caveat: the "pay on results" bullet is the current pilot terms (memory/ria-offer-locked-terms.md: $1k/household + 10% first-year fees, then $5k/mo from day 30). Keep the public line as "no cost to you, no retainers, small fee per closed client"; exact numbers stay for call 2.
