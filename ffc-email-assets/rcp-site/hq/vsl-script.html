Source: Hormozi bot, relayed by Michael 2026-11-12. Verbatim. 7–10 minutes, talking head + a few simple slides. Sent in the confirmation email the same day as the intro call. The closing call assumes they watched it.

It does three things: edify Michael, show the system, pre-sell the pilot so the close is a formality.

Hook (30–45s)

Michael (2026-11-13): starts with their name. It has to feel like a personal Loom, not a produced video.

"Hey [Name]. Thanks for hopping on that quick intro call.

You're getting this video because you run an independent RIA in the [100–250] million range, you serve pre-retirees and retirees, and you told me you're open to adding more good households without more steak dinners or lead-vendor roulette.

In the next 7–10 minutes I'll walk you through: - The exact 'Retirement Class' funnel we'd install for you - The numbers it's built to produce - And how the 30-day founding pilot works, where we only get paid when you add new $500k+ households."

Problem (1–2 min)

"Every firm I talk to says some version of: - 'Referrals are great but lumpy.' - 'Dinners and seminars are expensive and hit-or-miss.' - 'Lead lists are full of people who ghost the second they hear our minimums.'

The result is a roller coaster: some months you're slammed, others the calendar is light and it gnaws at you because you know every year without intentional growth makes it harder to hit your long-term goals."

Solution overview (2–3 min)

"Instead of chasing tactics, we install one education-first system under your brand: - A branded online 'Retirement Tax & Income Class' you record once. - Ads that invite 55–75 year-olds with $500k+ in investable assets in your geography into that class. - We'll install 24/7 specialists that will call, text, follow up with, nurture, educate and qualify, and get you set up. A follow-up process that filters for the right households and books them straight onto your calendar for a 45-minute 'Retirement Map Call.'

You stay in your lane: educate, then plan one-on-one. We live in the weeds of funnels, copy, and numbers."

Numbers (2–3 min)

"Let's talk brass tacks.

Your typical client: household assets [insert range you learned], fee around 1% blended on the first chunk of assets. So a $1M client is roughly $10,000/year in advisory fees, and over a 10-year relationship that's easily low-six-figures in revenue.

Our system is built to: - Spend real money to get qualified retirees into your class - Optimize around cost per qualified booked meeting with 500k+ or 1M+ households - And let you do what you already do best: convert trust into long-term relationships

If we add even a handful of the right households each quarter, the math works extremely well for a firm your size."

Pilot offer (2–3 min)

"Because this is a new distribution model for this niche, we're only installing it with a small number of founding firms.

For those firms, the 30-day pilot looks like this: - You cover ad spend. - We build and install the full funnel on your domain. - We write your class and ad scripts. - We set up and manage the campaigns and the qualification flow. - You record the class and take the meetings.

There's no setup fee and no management retainer during the pilot.

We only earn when you do: $1,000 for each new household with $500k+ that comes through this funnel and hires your firm, plus 10% of the first-year advisory fees on those assets.

If after 30 days you've seen at least 5 qualified meetings and like the direction, we talk about continuing on a simple monthly + success-fee structure. If not, you keep the funnel and data, and we shake hands."

Call to action (30–60s)

"On our next call, I'm going to walk you through the version of this funnel we've already mocked up with your name, firm, and city on it, and we'll plug your numbers into this model.

Come to that call ready with: - How many new households you added in the last 12 months, - Your typical AUM per new client, - And what 'good' would look like over the next year.

Then we can make an adult decision together: either this is the growth lever you've been missing, or it's not. Either way, no hard feelings.

Talk soon."

The flow (Hormozi)

Setter books 5-min intro. You run the quick "are you even open to this?" call. Send VSL same day. Next call you just confirm fit, walk funnel, and say, "Do you want one of the pilot slots or not?" That's the whole game.
