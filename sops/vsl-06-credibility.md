# VSL Section 6 SOP — Credibility

> **When to load:** VSL Section 6 (after the Method).
> **What it does:** the founder's backstory — Empathy ("I was where you are") → Discovery ("what I cracked") → Authority ("the proof I can help").
> **Output:** a TIGHT 3-beat first-person story, pushed to the "Credibility" card.
> **Pulls from:** OUTSIDE the system — the user's own story. So you INTERVIEW them first, then write.

## This section is different: INTERVIEW first, then write. Do NOT push on the first turn.
Sections 1–5 wrote straight from the board. This one is the founder's real story — you can't invent it. So on the first turn you **ask a few sharp questions**, wait for their answer, THEN craft the story and push it. (You already know the avatar's pains and their vehicle, so make the questions specific — don't ask blank generic ones.)

## Phase 1 — Ask (first turn: questions only, NO push)
Open with the framing: *"Now Section 6: Credibility. This is your story — the 'I was you, then I cracked it' moment that makes them trust you. I just need a few things from you, then I'll shape it into a tight, powerful story."*

Then ask these — warm and specific, grounded in what you already know about their world. Ask all four in ONE friendly message (not one at a time), and tell them a rough sentence each is plenty:
1. 👋 **Who are you?** (first name — and I'll write this in first person as you.)
2. 😣 **Before this worked, what were you doing** — were you grinding the same way your audience is now? *(mirror their Step 3 pains — e.g. "chasing it the hard way, barely scraping results?")*
3. 💡 **What was the turning point / epiphany** — what did you figure out or discover that changed everything? *(this becomes the Discovery — tie it to your vehicle/mechanism.)*
4. 🏆 **What proof do you have** — a specific result (a number), and ideally that it worked across more than one situation/person (so it reads as "not luck")?

**Get all 4 before you craft — people reply in different ways, handle both:**
- **All at once** (they dump the whole story in one message) → perfect, don't re-ask anything, go straight to Phase 2 and write it.
- **In parts / a one-liner / rambling** → warmly acknowledge what you got and nudge only for what's still missing: *"Love it 👍 — got your story and the turning point. Two quick ones: what were you doing BEFORE, and do you have a number you're proud of?"* Keep track of which of the 4 you still have, and ask ONLY for those. Never re-ask something they already answered.
- If they already told you some of this earlier in the chat, don't re-ask — just confirm what you have.

Don't write the story until you actually have all 4 (or the user clearly says *"just go with what you've got"*). A missing beat = a weaker story, so it's worth the quick nudge — but stay light and encouraging, never an interrogation. The moment you have enough, move to Phase 2.

## Phase 2 — Craft + push (once they answer)
Take their answers and write the TIGHT 3-beat story:
1. 🤝 **Empathy** *(you were them)* → "For years, I **[did it the way they do now ← mirror their pain]**. It **[was okay / modest result]**."
2. 💡 **Discovery** *(turning point)* → "Then I **[cracked / discovered ← the mechanism / vehicle]**."
3. 🏆 **Authority** *(proof + bridge)* → "And it **[big specific result]** — across **[range → proves it's not luck]**. So whether you're **[avatar ← Step 2]**, this works."

Then push it (this is the direct-push part — don't ask permission to push once you've written it):
- Short confirmation FIRST (visible), then the `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` markers.
- Visible line: *"Done — your story's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 7 (Proof)?"*

### Push content format (renders clean on the card)
```
[The tight 3-beat first-person story — a short paragraph, not a resume.]
```

## Rules
- **First person, ALWAYS** — introduce their name once, never "he/she/they."
- Keep it **TIGHT** — credibility is the boring part; hit all 3 beats in as few words as possible. A short paragraph, not a page.
- **Conversational, not resume-style.**
- Mirror the Empathy beat to the avatar's real pains (so they see themselves in it).
- If they don't have a big number yet, use the most honest specific result they DO have — never fabricate proof. If they have none at all, keep Authority modest and true, and note they can strengthen it later.
- 3rd-grade words.

## Quality test
Does a reader think *"this person was exactly where I am — and they figured it out"*? If it reads like a bragging résumé, or if the reader can't see themselves in the Empathy beat — rewrite tighter.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Interviewed the user first (didn't invent the story).
- [ ] 3 beats present: Empathy → Discovery → Authority.
- [ ] First person, name introduced once.
- [ ] Empathy mirrors the avatar's real pains; Discovery ties to the vehicle/mechanism.
- [ ] Proof is specific and TRUE (not fabricated); reads as "not luck" if possible.
- [ ] Tight, conversational, 3rd-grade level; flows from the Method.
