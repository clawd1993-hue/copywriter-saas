# Webinar Section 2 SOP — Credibility (Origin Story — Full Epiphany Bridge)

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 2 (right after the Hook). Brunson puts the origin story early.
> **What it does:** the trust engine. Right after the Hook they're asking *"why should I listen to you?"* — this answers it as a **STORY, not a resume.** Full epiphany bridge: empathy → discovery → authority, told as a journey.
> **Output:** the spoken origin story, pushed to the "Credibility (Origin Story)" card.
> **Approach:** INTERVIEW first, then write. This is the founder's real story — you can't invent it.
> **Pulls from:** OUTSIDE the system (the user's story) — but points them to Step 3 (Failed Attempts) + Step 6 (Vehicle) for the words.

## This section INTERVIEWS first — do NOT write until you have the story.
Ask the questions, gather the answers, THEN write the epiphany bridge.

## Phase 1 — Ask (questions first, NO push)
Open with the framing: *"Now Section 2: your Origin Story. Right after the hook, they're thinking 'okay, but who are YOU?' This is where we earn their trust — as a story, not a résumé. I need your real journey, then I'll shape it into the epiphany bridge. A few questions:"*

Ask these (grounded — reference their board where noted). Handle answers **all-at-once or in parts**: track what you still have, chase only what's missing, don't write until you've got enough (or they say "just go").
1. 👇 **Backstory / low point** — *"Where did you START? The low point — the humbler and more relatable, the better (broke, stuck, clueless). The lower it starts, the more they believe 'if they could, I can.'"*
2. 😣 **The old way that failed** — *"From your Step 3 Failed Attempts, which were YOU doing yourself — the organic grind, the shiny objects, the tactics that never worked?"* (These are the SAME things the audience is stuck on now → the wall lands as "that's me.")
3. 🧱 **The wall** — *"What was the WALL — the exact moment the old way clearly failed you?"*
4. 💡 **The discovery** — *"What did you DISCOVER and build to get out?"* (This is your **Vehicle — Step 6** if you need the words.)
5. 🏆 **The proof** — *"Results in numbers — ideally across different situations. Your best testimonials / case studies."*
6. ❤️ **The why** — *"WHY do you now put this in other people's hands?"* (Becomes your closing line + the bridge onward.)

## Phase 2 — Write the epiphany bridge (once you have the answers)
Write it as a **STORY, not a resume** — two beats:
1. **What you believed / tried** — the old way you bought into (the one THEY still believe) — **and why it turned out NOT to be true** (the wall, the myth busted).
2. **What's actually true / what worked** — the discovery + what changed — **backed with proof.**

**Map the answers to the 7-step Epiphany Bridge:** Backstory (Q1) → Wall (Q2/Q3) → Epiphany (Q4) → Plan (Q4) → Conflict (the messy middle) → Achievement (Q5 proof) → Transformation (Q6 / who you are now).

Then push it (direct-push once written; don't ask permission; don't reprint in chat).
- Visible line: *"Done — your Origin Story's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 3 (Future Pace)?"*

## The 3 drills (bake these in)
- **Story, not CV.** Listing credentials repels — it makes you the untouchable guru. The story makes you *them, a few years ago.* Lead with the low point.
- **The Wall = their belief.** The false belief you bought into must be the EXACT one the audience holds now → "that's me" → they lean in.
- **"If I could, anyone can."** The humbler the start, the more it pre-dissolves the "I'm not smart/experienced enough" objection before a secret even touches it.

## Story boundary
§2 = the ONE big origin story — how YOU discovered the **vehicle**. The Secret stories (§6/7/8) are smaller, each about ONE belief. **Don't blow the whole story here.**

## Spoken example (match this voice)
> "I won't bore you with the whole sob story — but a few years ago I was [low point]… I believed [old way]… until [wall]… then I discovered [epiphany]… and that changed everything. [proof across situations]."

## Rules
- **First person.** A real turning point. **Story, not resume.**
- End on **WHY you built this** → bridges into the dream/secrets.
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade; don't fabricate proof — use the real results (or honest placeholders clearly marked).

## Quality test
Do they think *"they've been where I am — and if they did it, I can too"*? If it reads like a résumé or a guru flex, rewrite it as a story.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Interviewed first (didn't invent the story).
- [ ] Told as a STORY (epiphany bridge), first person, leads with the low point.
- [ ] The Wall = the exact belief the audience still holds; proof included.
- [ ] Ends on WHY → hands into Future Pace. Doesn't blow the secret stories.
- [ ] Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.
