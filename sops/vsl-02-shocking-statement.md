# VSL Section 2 SOP — The Shocking Statement

> **When to load:** VSL Section 2 (right after the Hook).
> **What it does:** write the pattern-interrupt line that snaps a cold viewer out of autopilot and makes them lean in.
> **Output:** one startling line (two max), pushed to the "Shocking Statement" card.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 2: the Shocking Statement. The Hook stopped their scroll — this line snaps them out of autopilot and makes them lean in. One job: challenge a belief they hold, or say out loud the thing they secretly suspect but nobody's said. Then they HAVE to keep watching."* Then write it.

## What it is
*(Jim Edwards' VSL Part 1.)* A startling, pattern-interrupt line. It either **challenges a belief they hold** or **says out loud something they already suspect is true.** Its only job is to stop them and make them lean in. *(The deep pain-agitation comes later in Section 4 — this is just the jolt. Edwards = Problem → Agitate → Solve.)*

## Grounding (pull from the board — never invent)
Pull from **Step 3 Research** — pick ONE angle:
- 🔄 **A belief to challenge** ← Step 3 **Objections** — what they wrongly assume (e.g. *"the winners just have better offers"*), OR
- 👀 **A truth to expose** ← Step 3 **Pains / Failures** — the thing they quietly suspect but haven't heard said out loud (e.g. *"worse offers than yours are winning"*).

Also read **the VSL so far** (the Hook) so this line **flows and doesn't repeat it** — it's a fresh angle, not a restatement.

## The shapes (pick the one that hits hardest — each is a scaffold, not a script)
- 🔄 **Bold claim / reframe** — *"It's not [what they assume] — it's [the surprising real thing]."* → "It's not a better offer — it's an 8-step checklist."
- ❓ **Did-you-know** — *"Did you know [surprising fact they suspect]?"*
- 📊 **Stat** — *"[number] and [zero / worse result]."* → "$7k on ads, zero sales."
- ⚖️ **Comparison** — *"You don't [flattering thing they think], you [harsher truth]."* → "You don't own a business, you own a job."

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
The Shocking Statement card is **editable**, so DON'T hold it in the chat for approval and DON'T ask permission to push. *(Overrides the usual "always ask" rule.)*
1. Open with the framing above (a line or two).
2. Pick the angle + shape from the research, write the ONE best line.
3. **Immediately PUSH it** to the card, wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>`. Short confirmation FIRST (visible), then the markers.
4. Visible line: **don't re-print it, don't ask permission** — hand them the fork: *"Done — your Shocking Statement's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 3 (Why — the Desire)?"*

*(Only if they ASK for options: give 2–3 in chat and push their pick. Default = push the one best line.)*

### Push content format (renders clean on the card)
```
[The final startling line — one sentence, two max.]
```

## Rules
- ONE line, maybe two. **3rd-grade words.** A pattern-interrupt, not a paragraph.
- Must EITHER challenge a belief OR say the thing they suspect but nobody's said out loud.
- Don't restate the Hook — new angle.
- Don't agitate the pain in full here — that's Section 4. This is just the jolt.
- Use the avatar's own language from the research.

## Quality test
Does it *snap them out of autopilot*? If they'd just nod and scroll past, it's not startling enough — sharpen it.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Pulled from the REAL Step 3 research (an objection to flip or a pain/failure to expose).
- [ ] Reads the Hook so it flows and doesn't repeat it.
- [ ] One line (two max), 3rd-grade level, pattern-interrupt.
- [ ] Challenges a belief OR voices a suspected truth.
- [ ] Passes the "snap out of autopilot" test.
