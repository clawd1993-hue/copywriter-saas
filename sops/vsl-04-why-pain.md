# VSL Section 4 SOP — Why (The Pain)

> **When to load:** VSL Section 4 (after the Desire scene).
> **What it does:** agitate the cost of doing nothing — make where they are, and where it's headed, feel real and urgent.
> **Output:** a 2–3 paragraph agitation that ENDS on a turning-point line, pushed to the "Why (Pain)" card.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 4: Why — the Pain. We showed them the dream. Now we make staying put feel unbearable. This is the agitate beat — honest pressure on where they are now and where it's headed if nothing changes — then one line that flips to hope and sets up the Method."* Then write it.

## What it is
*(Jim Edwards' Agitate beat.)* Agitate the cost of doing nothing — make the weight of where they are *now*, and where it's headed, feel real and urgent. Honest pressure, not manufactured fear.

## Grounding (pull from Step 3 Research — never invent)
Write it as **3 beats** (each says where it comes from):
1. 😣 **The present pain** ← Step 3 **Pains** — name where they are *right now*, in their own words. *(Optional: name what they've already tried that still didn't fix it ← Step 3 **Failures** — deepens the "stuck" feeling.)*
2. ⏳ **The cost of staying** — pick ONE agitation angle:
   - 📉 **Trajectory** ← Step 3 **Time Dimensions (Future fears)** — where they'll be in 6 months / 2 years if nothing changes.
   - 🏃 **FOMO / urgency** ← Step 3 **Motivations (competitive)** — others are pulling ahead (even with less); the window's closing.
3. 🔄 **The turning point** — one line that flips to hope and hands off to the Method: *"But it doesn't have to stay this way."*

Also read **the VSL so far** — this is the FLIP side of Section 3's desire. Don't repeat those lines; agitate the absence of that dream.

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
The Why (Pain) card is **editable**, so DON'T hold it in the chat for approval and DON'T ask permission to push. *(Overrides the usual "always ask" rule.)*
1. Open with the framing above (a line or two).
2. Write the 3-beat agitation — 2–3 short paragraphs — and **always end on the turning-point line**.
3. **Immediately PUSH it** to the card, wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>`. Short confirmation FIRST (visible), then the markers.
4. Visible line: **don't re-print it, don't ask permission** — hand them the fork: *"Done — your Pain section's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 5 (Introduce the Method)?"*

### Push content format (renders clean on the card)
```
[The 2–3 paragraph agitation, ending on the turning-point line.]
```

## Rules
- Be **HONEST** — agitate what they *already* feel; don't manufacture fear.
- Use their **own words** from the research.
- 2–3 short paragraphs.
- **Always end on the turning-point line** (the bridge to Section 5, the Method).
- Don't re-run the desire from Section 3 — this is its shadow.
- 3rd-grade words.

## Quality test
Does it make them uncomfortable staying put? If they'd read it and shrug — it's too gentle. Sharpen it.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Names the present pain in the avatar's own words (← Pains, optional Failures).
- [ ] One clear cost-of-staying angle (← Future fears OR competitive FOMO).
- [ ] Ends on the turning-point line that bridges to the Method.
- [ ] Honest agitation, not manufactured fear.
- [ ] 2–3 short paragraphs, 3rd-grade level; doesn't repeat Section 3.
