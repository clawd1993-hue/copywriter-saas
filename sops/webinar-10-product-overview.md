# Webinar Section 10 SOP — Product Overview ("here's the thing I built")

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 10 (right after the Transition / permission-to-sell). The on-ramp to the stack.
> **What it does:** name the flagship, hit the Big Promise, hand into §11 (The Stack). **Lean** — the value reveal is §11, NOT here.
> **Output:** the flagship name + Big Promise + hand-off, pushed to the "Product Overview" card.
> **Pulls from:** **Step 8.1 — the flagship NAME + the 🚗 Big Promise lead-in.** *(This is where the actual PRODUCT they buy finally gets named — the vehicle/beliefs were handled in §4-8.)*

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 10: Product Overview. They said yes to seeing it — so here we name the actual thing they get and hit the big promise, then hand straight into everything that's inside. Keep it lean; the full value reveal is the next section."* Then write it.

## How to write it (lean — name + promise only)
1. **Name the flagship** ← Step 8.1 (the container / product name). This is the reveal — the branded name shows up HERE for the first time.
2. **The 🚗 Big Promise** ← Step 8.1: dream + without [failures] + even-if [nothing works].
3. **Hand off** into the stack: *"Here's everything you get…"*

## Spoken example (match this voice)
> "So here's what I built: **[Flagship name]**. When you join, you'll finally [dream] — without [failures] — even if [nothing's worked before]. Let me show you everything you get…"

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat. Solo.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- Visible line: *"Done — your Product Overview's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 11 (The Stack — where we reveal everything they get)?"*

## Rules
- **Lean. Name + promise only.** **NO component list, NO dollar values here** (that's §11).
- This is the ONE place the branded flagship **name** is revealed — use it.
- End by handing into the stack.
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Do they get what it IS, and want to see inside? If you started listing components or values, stop — that's §11.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Names the flagship (← Step 8.1) — the branded name reveal.
- [ ] Hits the 🚗 Big Promise (dream + without + even-if).
- [ ] Lean — no component list, no values.
- [ ] Hands into The Stack; spoken tone, 3rd-grade.
