# Webinar Section 18 SOP — CTA (the direct ask + two-choices close)

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 18 (right after the Re-Stack).
> **What it does:** the direct ask — ONE clear action — then Brunson's "two choices" close.
> **Output:** the CTA, pushed to the "CTA" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 3 (the stuck old-world pains + the dream) for the two-choices contrast. OUTSIDE = the checkout link + exact price + button text.

## This section ASKS first (needs link + price + button text). Do NOT push on the first turn.
The link, price, and button text are the user's — you can't invent them.

## Phase 1 — Ask (first turn: get the details, NO push)
Open with the framing: *"Now Section 18: the CTA — one clear action, then the two-choices close. Quick, I just need the real details:"* Then ask (handle all-at-once or in parts):
1. 🔘 **The action** — "What exactly do they do? (click the button below / go to [link])"
2. 💵 **The price** — confirm the number they're paying (if not already set from §14).
3. 🖱️ **Button text** — "What should the button say? (e.g. 'Yes — I'm In', 'Join Now')" (if they don't care, recommend one.)

## Phase 2 — Write (once you have the details)
Three beats:
1. **Tell them exactly what to do** — *"click the button below"* (ONE action, unmistakable).
2. **Restate the core benefit + price** — the transformation for $[price].
3. **The two-choices close** ← Step 3 — *leave and nothing changes* (paint the stuck old-world — real Step 3 pains) **vs** *click and [transformation ← the dream].*

## Spoken example (match this voice)
> "So here's what to do: click the button right below this video to get [Vehicle] for just $[price]. Really, you've got two choices right now. You can close this page and nothing changes — [stuck old-world pain from Step 3]. Or you can click that button, and [the transformation / dream]. The choice is yours — but I know which one I'd make."

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push once you have the details. Don't reprint in chat.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- End handing into the future-pace: *"So picture this…"*
- Visible line: *"Done — your CTA's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 19 (the Future-Pace Close)?"*

## Rules
- **ONE action only** — unmistakable next step.
- Restate benefit + price.
- **End on the two-choices closing question** (stuck ← Step 3 pains vs transformation ← the dream).
- No new info — the offer's already made.
- Hand into the Future-Pace Close.
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Is the next step unmistakable, and does the two-choices close make staying put feel like the painful option?

## Quality gate
- [ ] Asked for link + price + button text first (didn't invent).
- [ ] ONE clear action; restates benefit + price.
- [ ] Two-choices close: stuck old-world (Step 3 pains) vs transformation (the dream).
- [ ] Hands into the Future-Pace Close; spoken tone, 3rd-grade.
