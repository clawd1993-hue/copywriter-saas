# Webinar Section 4 SOP — Set the Frame

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 4 (the LAST beat before the teaching starts).
> **What it does:** Brunson's frame beat. Reframe what's coming as ONE *precise* new opportunity (not the vague/generic thing they'd dismiss), then lock in a stay-with-me commitment. Primes them for the secrets.
> **Output:** the New-Opportunity Frame + a micro-commitment, pushed to the "Set the Frame" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 6 (Vehicle / new opportunity) + the Big Domino from §1. No interview.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 4: Set the Frame — the last thing before we teach. This is where we reframe what's coming as ONE precise new opportunity, so they don't lump it in with every generic thing they've dismissed before. Then we lock them in for the whole session."* Then write it.

## ⭐ THE CORE MOVE — the New-Opportunity Frame (ALWAYS lead with this) — a 3-part contrast
1. **What it's NOT** → dismiss the generic bucket they'd lump it into (*"this isn't theory… it's not some generic sales training… it's not the old grind of posting and praying."*).
2. **What it IS** → the **precise, specific new opportunity** (← Step 6), **named for their niche** (*"an extremely precise system for booking paid [niche] clients"*).
3. **The specific hard outcome** → the almost-hard-to-believe result it produces (*"…that gets total strangers to book you and pay a deposit before they've even met you."*).

⭐ **"Precise / specific" is the lever** — it's what separates you from every vague guru pitch and makes this feel like a real, NEW mechanism. **Never skip the "what it's NOT" half** — the dismissal is what makes the "what it IS" land as new.

> ⚠️ **Brand-name rule (same as the Hook):** "what it IS" = the precise new **mechanism / system / concept**, named for the niche — **NOT the branded product/offer name** (that's held for §10/11). Say "a precise system for booking paid shoots," not "The 90-Day Paid Portrait Pro Blueprint."

## Then close the frame with a micro-commitment
*"So keep an open mind and stay locked in the whole way through."* (open-mind mindset + stay-to-end, in one line.)

## ⚠️ Keep it TIGHT + non-redundant
- **DON'T re-state the Big Domino in full** (that was §1) — reinforce it, don't repeat it.
- **DON'T name the 3 secrets** (that's §5).
- **Optional** light bridge to §5: *"there are just 3 things standing between you and this…"* — the **promise only, never the content.**

## Spoken example (match this voice)
> "This isn't theory, and it's not some generic 'grow your following' advice. It's a precise, specific system for booking paid [niche] clients — one that gets total strangers to reach out and pay you a deposit before they've even met you. So keep an open mind and stay locked in the whole way through."

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat. Solo — no interview.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- Visible line: *"Done — your Frame's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 5 (the 3 Secrets Intro)?"*

## Rules
- **ALWAYS lead with the New-Opportunity Frame** (not-X → precise new opportunity → hard outcome). **Precision is the lever.**
- Name the precise **concept/mechanism**, NOT the branded product name.
- End on **open-mind / stay-locked-in.**
- **Reinforce (don't repeat) the domino; don't name the secrets.**
- Tight — this is a short beat. Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Do they think *"oh — this is precise and specific, not the usual fluff. Show me."*? If it sounds like generic guru framing, sharpen the "what it IS" until it's precise and niche-specific.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Leads with the New-Opportunity Frame: what it's NOT → the precise new opportunity (concept, niche-named) → the hard outcome.
- [ ] Uses the precise mechanism/concept, NOT the branded product name.
- [ ] Ends on the open-mind / stay-locked-in micro-commitment.
- [ ] Doesn't re-state the full Big Domino or name the 3 secrets; tight.
