# VSL Section 9 SOP — The Pitch

> **When to load:** VSL Section 9 (after Product Overview).
> **What it does:** the offer reveal — "here's exactly what you get." Reveal the CORE offer exactly as Step 8.1 already built it.
> **Output:** the core offer revealed as 3 deliverables — the 🚗 flagship (a tangible roadmap in the lead-in) + the 🧠/🌍 component bullets with values — pushed to the "Pitch" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 8.1 — the core, already packaged. You REPRODUCE it in reveal form. You do NOT re-count, re-pick, invent, or promote anything.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 9: the Pitch. Section 8 made them believe the opportunity is real — this is where we show them exactly what they get. It's your core offer, revealed piece by piece, each one killing a doubt as it lands."* Then write it.

## ⚠️ The core comes from Step 8.1 — reveal it as 3 REAL things they get (don't rebuild it)
The core was locked in Step 8.1. Your job is to reveal it as **three tangible deliverables** — #1 the flagship, #2 and #3 the components:
- **🚗 = the flagship — a NAMED, TANGIBLE deliverable** (a step-by-step **roadmap / blueprint / system / plan**) that delivers the dream. Present it IN the lead-in as **Deliverable #1** — a concrete thing they GET, phrased *"When you join [Vehicle], you'll get a step-by-step [roadmap] to [dream]…"* — **NOT** a vague *"you'll finally [dream]."* The flagship is a thing, not an airy promise.
- **🧠 = component deliverable #2** (a Step 5 mechanism), with its value.
- **🌍 = component deliverable #3** (a Step 5 mechanism), with its value.

**Pull the 🧠/🌍 bullets from Step 8.1 verbatim** — same names, same values. The flagship's name + dream also come from Step 8.1; you simply frame it as the tangible roadmap. Do NOT:
- ❌ invent a new deliverable or promote a bonus into the core to hit a count,
- ❌ re-map deliverables to Method moves,
- ❌ change the 🧠/🌍 names or values.

If Step 8.1's core is a flagship + 2 component bullets, that's your pitch: roadmap (Deliverable #1) + the 2 bullets. Mirror it.

## Grounding (pull from the Step 8 card — never invent)
- Read the **8.1 Core** off the Step 8 Deliverables card (← Step 8, in the board context).
- Use the **exact names, bullets, and dollar values** from Step 8.1 for the 🧠/🌍 components. The **flagship (roadmap) carries a value only if Step 8.1 gave it one** — otherwise leave it valueless; the two components carry the values.
- Also read **the VSL so far** — flow out of Section 8's hand-off, and keep the "even if"s here MICRO (deliverable-specific), not a repeat of Section 8's macro belief-breaks.

1. Open with the framing above (a line or two).
2. Reveal the flagship as the tangible roadmap (lead-in) → "And you'll get…" → the 🧠/🌍 bullets from Step 8.1.

## Rules
- **Reveal 3 real deliverables:** the 🚗 **flagship** = a tangible roadmap/blueprint (Deliverable #1, named in the lead-in — a thing, not a vague "you'll finally…"), then the 🧠/🌍 component bullets. Transition into them with **"And you'll get…"**.
- **Mirror the 🧠/🌍 bullets from Step 8.1 — never re-count, invent, or promote.**
- Each component bullet uses the **full Edwards formula** (so you can… even if… which means…) + its value.
- The "even if"s are **MICRO** (deliverable-specific) — don't echo Section 8's macro belief-breaks verbatim.
- Values carry the ones Step 8.1 assigned (they feed Section 10's total-value math).
- **Conversational reveal, not a robotic claim list.**
- 3rd-grade words.
