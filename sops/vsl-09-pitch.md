# VSL Section 9 SOP — The Pitch

> **When to load:** VSL Section 9 (after Product Overview).
> **What it does:** the offer reveal — "here's exactly what you get." Reveal the CORE offer exactly as Step 8.1 already built it.
> **Output:** the core offer (🚗 Big-Promise lead-in + 🧠/🌍 component bullets, with values), pushed to the "Pitch" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 8.1 — the core, already packaged. You REPRODUCE it in reveal form. You do NOT re-count, re-pick, invent, or promote anything.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 9: the Pitch. Section 8 made them believe the opportunity is real — this is where we show them exactly what they get. It's your core offer, revealed piece by piece, each one killing a doubt as it lands."* Then write it.

## ⚠️ The core is ALREADY BUILT in Step 8.1 — mirror it, don't rebuild it
This is the rule that keeps the Pitch consistent every time. The core was **belief-mapped and locked in Step 8.1**. Your job is to REVEAL it, not re-derive it:
- **🚗 = the Big Promise / the vehicle itself** — written as the **lead-in** ("When you join [Vehicle], you'll finally [dream]… here's everything you get:"). It is the umbrella, **NOT** a peer product bullet.
- **🧠 = one named component bullet** (a Step 5 mechanism), with its value.
- **🌍 = one named component bullet** (a Step 5 mechanism), with its value.

So the core = **1 Big-Promise lead-in + the component bullets that sit under it** — exactly as they appear on the Step 8 "Deliverables" card.

**Reproduce the Step 8.1 core VERBATIM (names, bullets, values). Do NOT:**
- ❌ expect or invent "3 equal named products,"
- ❌ promote a bonus into the core to hit a count,
- ❌ re-map deliverables to Method moves,
- ❌ add, drop, or renumber anything.

Whatever the Step 8 card shows as the **8.1 Core** IS your pitch. If it's a 🚗 lead-in + 2 bullets, that's the pitch. Mirror it.

## Grounding (pull from the Step 8 card — never invent)
- Read the **8.1 Core** off the Step 8 Deliverables card (← Step 8, in the board context).
- Use the **exact names, bullets, and dollar values** already assigned. If a value is genuinely missing, propose a believable one and flag it lightly — never silently change one.
- Also read **the VSL so far** — open on Section 8's hand-off, and keep the "even if"s here MICRO (deliverable-specific), not a repeat of Section 8's macro belief-breaks.

## The format (reveal it like showing someone the goods — not a slide)
```
Here's exactly what you get:

[🚗 Big-Promise lead-in — "When you join [Vehicle name], you'll finally [dream] — without [failures] — even if [nothing's worked]. Here's everything inside:"]

**[🧠 Name]** — [what it is] so you can [outcome], even if [objection], which means [payoff]. (Value: $X)

**[🌍 Name]** — [what it is] so you can [outcome], even if [objection], which means [payoff]. (Value: $X)

[optional close line: "That's the whole machine — and you're not building any of it."]
```
*(If Step 8.1 packaged more/fewer component bullets, mirror that — the structure follows the board, not a fixed number.)*

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
The Pitch card is **editable**, so DON'T ask permission — reproduce the core and push straight to the card. Per the section output contract, do NOT reprint it in the chat.
1. Open with the framing above (a line or two).
2. Reveal the core (🚗 lead-in + component bullets) exactly as Step 8.1 built it.
3. **Immediately PUSH it** wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>`. Short confirmation FIRST (visible), then the markers.
4. Visible line — the fork: *"Done — your Pitch's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 10 (the Bonuses — where we stack the free gifts on top)?"*

## Rules
- **Mirror Step 8.1 — never re-count, invent, or promote.** The 🚗 is the lead-in Big Promise, not a peer bullet.
- Each component bullet uses the **full Edwards formula** (so you can… even if… which means…) + its value.
- The "even if"s are **MICRO** (deliverable-specific) — don't echo Section 8's macro belief-breaks verbatim.
- Values carry the ones Step 8.1 assigned (they feed Section 10's total-value math).
- **Conversational reveal, not a robotic claim list.**
- 3rd-grade words.

## Quality test
Does the reveal match the core that's on the board — and does each piece make them think *"that alone is worth it"* while quietly killing a doubt? If you added, dropped, or renumbered anything vs Step 8.1 — you broke the rule; mirror it instead.
