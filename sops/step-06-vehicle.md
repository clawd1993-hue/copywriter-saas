# Step 6 SOP — Name the Vehicle (New Opportunity)

> **When to load:** Step 6, once Steps 1–5 are on the board.
> **What this step does:** Name + position the "Vehicle" (the new category the offer lives in), then write 3 belief-breaking secrets.
> **Output:** Vehicle Name + Positioning line + 3 Secrets (🚗 / 🧠 / 🌍).

## The big idea (frame it right for the user)
The **Vehicle** is the NEW OPPORTUNITY — the new *category* the offer lives in. It's NOT the product and NOT the steps. It's the thesis that makes the customer think *"oh, THAT's different from everything I've tried."* It quietly implies the old ways failed because they were the wrong CATEGORY, not because the customer executed badly. A 10-year-old should get it, it sounds proprietary, and it gives them a new identity to buy into.

## Grounding (pull from the board — never invent)
Everything here traces to real earlier steps already on the dashboard:
- **Step 2** = the audience.
- **Step 3** = Desires, Motivations, Objections, Failures (the real research).
- **Step 5** = the named solutions (your mechanism pool).

**⚠️ The 3 secrets are OBJECTION-driven (Step 3 Objections) — NOT the Step 4/5 journey/problem steps.** Do not build the secrets from the journey. Every blank must trace to a real Step 3 / Step 5 / Part A source. Nothing invented.

## How to run it (take the lead → propose → present for review)
Propose the WHOLE thing (name + positioning + 3 secrets) in one clean message, then invite tweaks. Keep the user-facing output **simple** — no worksheets, no source labels shown; you do the sourcing internally. This is NOT an interrogation and NOT auto-push — the user reviews before it's locked.

### PART A — Name + Positioning
1. **NAME** = the formula **"The [Timeframe] [Dream] [Format]"**
   - [Timeframe] = program length (90-Day / 6-Week / 30-Day — skip if there's no clear one)
   - [Dream] = the outcome in 1–2 words ← **Step 3 Desires**
   - [Format] = pick ONE: Blueprint · Challenge · Workshop · System · Method · Protocol · Roadmap · Formula · Plan
   - Test: *"I'm doing The [name]"* should sound like a real product, not a description.
2. **SUPERPOWER** = what YOUR thing gives them that the old stuff (← Step 3 Failures) doesn't — the new/better way (1–2 words / short phrase).
3. **POSITIONING** = **"The [Name] is the first [category] for [audience ← Step 2]. Unlike [the old ways ← Step 3 Failures], it [superpower]."**

### PART B — The 3 Secrets (belief-breaks)
Three one-liners, each killing ONE doubt that stops the buyer. The 3 objections must be genuinely different: 🚗 nothing-works · 🧠 about themselves · 🌍 about the outside world.
- 🚗 **Vehicle** (the Big Promise restated): *"How to **[DREAM ← S3 Motivations]** — without **[FAILURES ← S3]** — even if **[the 'nothing works' OBJECTION ← S3]** — using **[the VEHICLE ← Part A]**."*
- 🧠 **Internal:** *"How to **[opposite DESIRE ← S3]** — without **[FAILURE, internal ← S3]** — even if **[INTERNAL OBJECTION ← S3]** — with **[MECHANISM ← a S5 solution]**."*
- 🌍 **External:** *"How to **[opposite DESIRE ← S3]** — without **[FAILURE, external ← S3]** — even if **[EXTERNAL OBJECTION ← S3]** — with **[MECHANISM ← a S5 solution]**."*

**Rules for the secrets:**
- Each mechanism is **teased, not explained** (the reveal is Step 7). No two secrets share the same mechanism.
- Each should spark curiosity — *"wait, really? how?"*
- All three ladder up to the Big Promise (the positioning). They don't restate the whole dream.

## Delivering + push
1. Present the full draft in chat, clean and simple: the **Name**, the **Positioning** line, and the **3 Secrets** (🚗 / 🧠 / 🌍).
2. Invite quick tweaks: *"Want to adjust the name, the positioning, or any of the 3 secrets?"* Handle changes fast, then loop back.
3. Drive to the push: *"Want me to lock this to your dashboard?"* — on YES, output the final content wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>`.

### Push content format (renders clean on the card)
```
# Your Vehicle (New Opportunity)

## Name
**The [Name]**

## Positioning
"The [Name] is the first [category] for [audience]. Unlike [old ways], it [superpower]."

## The 3 Secrets
🚗 **Vehicle** — "How to [dream] — without [failures] — even if [objection] — using [vehicle]."
🧠 **Internal** — "How to [desire] — without [failure] — even if [objection] — with [mechanism]."
🌍 **External** — "How to [desire] — without [failure] — even if [objection] — with [mechanism]."
```

## ✅ Step 6 is DONE when
The Vehicle Name + Positioning + 3 Secrets are on the "Vehicle" card. Then ask: *"Ready for Step 7?"*

## Worked example (photography)
- **Name:** The 30-Day Paid Photographer Blueprint
- **Positioning:** "…the first income system for hobby photographers. Unlike pricey gear, film school, or shooting free for exposure, it gets you a client-ready portfolio and your first paid booking in 30 days."
- 🚗 "How to turn your camera into a real income — without pricey gear, film school, or years shooting free — even if you've tried to get clients before and nothing came of it — using the 30-Day Paid Photographer Blueprint."
- 🧠 "How to shoot photos clients happily pay for — without a decade of practice — even if you feel like just an amateur — with The Money-Shot Framework."
- 🌍 "How to get booked in a market full of iPhone 'photographers' — without slashing your prices — even if everyone says it's saturated — with The Stand-Out Portfolio."

## Quality gate (before you push)
- [ ] Name = "The [Timeframe] [Dream] [Format]"; Dream ← Step 3 Desires, Format from the list; sounds like a real product said out loud.
- [ ] Positioning names the audience (← Step 2) + contrasts the old ways (← Step 3 Failures) + states the superpower.
- [ ] 3 secrets built from Step 3 Objections (NOT the journey); the 3 objections are genuinely different (nothing-works / internal / external).
- [ ] 🚗 = dream (← S3 Motivations) + without failures + "nothing works" objection + the vehicle (← Part A).
- [ ] 🧠 / 🌍 mechanisms pulled from Step 5 solutions, teased not explained, no two the same.
- [ ] Every blank traces to a real source. Nothing invented.
