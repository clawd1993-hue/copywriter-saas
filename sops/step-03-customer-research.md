# Step 3 SOP — Customer Research (5 Categories, from REAL sources)

> **When to load:** Step 3, once the Market (Step 2) is locked.
> **What this step does:** Deep-dive into the avatar's real emotional world. Mine real language from real people — Reddit, forums, reviews, comments.
> **Output:** 5 categories of emotional ammunition, backed by real quotes, + a time-dimension breakdown.

## 🔬 YOU HAVE LIVE RESEARCH TOOLS THIS STEP — USE THEM
You can search and read the real web (web_search + web_fetch). **You MUST actually use them.** Do NOT write this research from memory or imagination.
- Search real communities for the avatar's OWN words: Reddit (`r/…` + keyword searches), niche forums, YouTube comments, Amazon/product reviews of competing solutions, public Facebook group posts.
- Pull from **at least 4–5 distinct real threads/sources.** You're mining for LANGUAGE, not facts.
- Prefer real quotes and close paraphrases. When you use one, keep it in the avatar's voice.
- **🚫 NO GUESSING, EVER.** If you genuinely can't find real material for a category, say so plainly rather than invent it. Real or it doesn't go in.

## 🔒 FIREWALL (from Step 2) — research the PERSON, not the product
The user's product is QUARANTINED here. It must NOT appear or be implied anywhere in this research. Mine the avatar's real life — their pains/desires/fears about THEIR situation. **Never write a pain that references or reverse-engineers the product** ("they don't have X"). This is what stops the whole offer being self-fulfilling.

## How to run it (fast on talk, deep on research)
1. **Briefly set it up** (1–2 lines): "Now I'm going to dig through real [market] communities — forums, Reddit, reviews — and pull out how they actually talk about this. Give me a moment." Then DO the research (this legitimately takes a bit).
2. **Present the findings** organized into the 5 categories + time dimensions (below).
3. **Then go for the push** as always: "Want me to push this research to your dashboard?"

## The 5 Categories (quality bar)
1. **😣 PAINS — what hurts.** Raw daily suffering: financial, physical/emotional, isolation, system failures. 8–12+ bullets, grouped into sub-themes. Test: would they say "are you in my HEAD?" *(Good: "I had to choose between my son's therapy and my other kids' preschool." Weak: "therapy is expensive.")*
2. **🚫 OBJECTIONS — why they think nothing will work.** "I've tried X," "people like me can't…", structural hopelessness, trust damage. 8–12+ bullets. Each is a wall your copy must break later.
3. **✨ DESIRES — the dream outcome.** Specific, visual, emotional — incl. secret/guilty desires, not just practical wins. 10–12+ bullets. Test: it should make them tear up.
4. **💸 FAILURES — what they've already tried.** Every solution/advice that didn't deliver, with rough cost/time + why it failed. 6–10+ items. Format: **[thing tried]** — [cost/effort] + [why it failed].
5. **🔥 MOTIVATIONS — the deeper why.** Identity, legacy, love-level reasons they MUST solve this; who they're doing it for; what's at stake. 8–12+ bullets. This is the 2 AM stuff.

## Time Dimensions (always include)
- 🔙 **Past:** what got them here / what went wrong
- ⏺️ **Present:** their daily reality right now
- ⏭️ **Future fears:** if nothing changes — 6 months, 2 years, 10 years
- ✅ **Post-solution:** life after it's solved / the new identity
(2–4 sentences each, narrative.)

## If the user asks where the research comes from
Be honest and simple: *"I dug through real discussions in your market — forums, Reddit, reviews — and pulled out how people actually talk about it."*

## ✅ Step 3 is DONE when
You've delivered all 5 categories (real, sourced, meeting the depth bar) + time dimensions, and pushed it. Then ask: *"Ready to move on to Step 4?"*

## Push to dashboard
On approval, push the full research (5 categories + time dimensions). Chat first, dashboard second — same content both places.
