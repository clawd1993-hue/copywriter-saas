# Webinar Section 15 SOP — Objection Crush ("I know what you're thinking…")

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 15 (right after the Price).
> **What it does:** say the top objections OUT LOUD before they harden, and kill each in 1–2 sentences. An unspoken objection quietly kills the sale.
> **Output:** the objection crush (each named + reframed), pushed to the "Objection Crush" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 3 objections + Step 4 problems (name them in THEIR words). Reframes = the offer + proof + guarantee.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 15: Objection Crush. Right after the price, their brain throws up every 'yeah but…' — so we say each one out loud before they can, and dissolve it in a sentence or two. Name it, reframe it, move on."* Then write it.

## Light ask first (one quick turn — then write)
Ask: *"Two quick things so this lands: (1) any proof/testimonials/results I can cite to kill the 'will it actually work' doubt? (if not, I'll lean on the guarantee) (2) any objection specific to YOUR audience or this launch that isn't already in your research?"* — take the answer (or "just go") and write.

## The universal objection checklist — crush each (1–2 sentences)
1. 💰 **Money / "can't afford it"** → the **ROI math from §14** — the cost of NOT fixing it dwarfs the price.
2. ⏰ **Time / "no time"** → it **saves** time (done-for-you / fast) — that's the whole point.
3. 🌍 **"Won't work for MY niche/situation"** → the universal mechanism + a couple of rotating examples across situations.
4. 🧠 **"I'm not smart/skilled enough"** → restate the **INTERNAL secret** — built for exactly them, no skill needed.
5. 🤨 **Skepticism / "is it legit / will it work"** → proof (if given) + the guarantee (hands toward §16).
6. ⏳ **"I'll do it later"** → the cost of waiting — the window's closing / competitors pulling ahead.
7. 🙋 **"Why you / why trust this"** → credibility (results, track record).
*(+ any project-specific objection straight from Step 3.)*

## How to write it
Each = one short beat: *"Now, maybe you're thinking [objection in THEIR words]… here's the thing: [1–2 sentence reframe]."* **Don't argue — reframe.** Use their real words from Step 3/4.

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push after the light ask. Don't reprint in chat.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- End handing into the guarantee: *"And to make this a total no-brainer…"*
- Visible line: *"Done — your Objection Crush is in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 16 (Guarantee & Urgency)?"*

## Rules
- **Name each objection before they do** — in their words (← Step 3/4).
- One short beat each — reframe, don't argue.
- Cover the 7 universal + any Step 3-specific objection.
- Lean on the guarantee for skepticism if there's no proof.
- Hand into Guarantee/Urgency.
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Is every top Step 3/4 objection **named AND answered**? Any objection left unspoken = a leak that kills the sale.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Asked for proof + any audience-specific objection first.
- [ ] Crushes the 7 universal objections + Step 3-specific ones, each named in their words + reframed in 1–2 sentences.
- [ ] Reframes (doesn't argue); leans on guarantee if no proof.
- [ ] Hands into Guarantee/Urgency; spoken tone, 3rd-grade.
