# Step 7 SOP — The Method (the simple path)

> **When to load:** Step 7, after Step 6 (Vehicle + 3 Secrets) is on the board.
> **What it does:** turns the Step 6 secrets into the simple **3-move path** the customer walks to the dream — written as a spoken script.
> **Output:** the Method, said out loud (7 beats). Becomes VSL Section 5 ("Introduce the Method").
> **Needs:** Step 6 done. **Nothing new to derive — the Method is built ENTIRELY from Step 6** (+ Step 3 Pains for the opening stakes).

## The big idea (frame it for the user)
The Method = your Step 6 secrets flipped from **belief → action**, landing on the dream. It's the SIMPLE path that makes the buyer think *"wait… that's it? I can do that."* Do NOT go back to Step 4/5 — the journey already did its job (that becomes the Step 8 bonus stack). Build only from Step 6.

## Where each move comes from
- 🧠 **Internal secret → Move 1** (an action — "you can learn to X")
- 🌍 **External secret → Move 2** (an action)
- 🚗 **Vehicle secret → Move 3 = the DREAM** (the landing — 🚗 is the whole vehicle, so it gives the dream, not a single tactic)

## The 7 beats — write it as a flowing spoken script
Pull every blank from **Step 6** (+ Step 3 Pains for the stakes). Easy verbs, 3rd-grade, short spoken sentences — read it aloud; if you stumble, cut it.

1. **Stakes ← Step 3 Pains:** "Right now you're **[pain]**. And it's hard."
2. **The turn ← timeframe (from the Vehicle name):** "But in the next **[timeframe]**…" *(let it trail off — do NOT add "you can turn it around" or any filler tail.)*
3. **Move 1 ← 🧠 internal secret (starts with "You can"):** "You can learn to **[🧠 opposite desire]** — without **[the old failed way in the 🧠 secret]**." *(No "First,". It flows straight out of the "…next [timeframe]…" line.)*
4. **Move 2 ← 🌍 external secret:** "Then, you can learn to **[🌍 opposite desire]** — without **[the old failed way in the 🌍 secret]**."
5. **Move 3 = the dream ← 🚗:** "And soon, you **[🚗 dream]**." *(plain + emotional, not a tactic)*
6. **Even-if close ← 🧠 + 🌍 objections:** "And you can do this even if **[🧠 objection]**. Even if **[🌍 objection]**."
7. **Because + proof ← superpower + mechanisms + proof + result:** "That's because you can follow **[the new/better way ← Step 6 superpower]** — using **[🧠 mechanism]** and **[🌍 mechanism]** — the same simple system others have already used to **[the result/dream ← 🚗]**."

## Rules
- Moves carry **outcome + "without [old failed way]"** — but **NO mechanisms in the moves.** The mechanisms come back in the "because" (🧠 mechanism answers the 🧠 even-if, 🌍 mechanism answers the 🌍 even-if).
- **NO vehicle/brand name anywhere in the Method** — the because = the new WAY + the two mechanisms + who's used it + the result; never the product name (that's Step 8 / the pitch).
- **Move 3 lands on the DREAM** — plain + emotional.
- **Easy verbs** (learn, do, get — not master/develop). 3rd-grade, spoken.

## Proof — DON'T fabricate (grounding rule)
The "because" wants proof ("hundreds of people have used this"). **NEVER invent specific numbers or names.** Default to honest soft proof: *"the same simple system others have already used to [result]."* If real proof would strengthen it, ask ONE quick question — *"Got any real numbers or names I can plug in (e.g. X students, a specific result)?"* — and use whatever they give. No made-up figures, ever.

## Delivering + push
1. Draft the full Method (the 7-beat spoken script) in chat.
2. Invite a quick tweak: *"Want to adjust any of it, or add real proof numbers?"* Handle changes fast.
3. Drive to the push: *"Want me to lock this to your dashboard?"* — on YES, write a short confirmation + **"Ready for Step 8?"** FIRST, then the content wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>`.

### Push content format (renders clean on the card)
```

[the full 7-beat spoken script, as flowing lines — the exact words they'd say]
```

## Worked example (photography)
> Right now you're shooting friends for free while people with worse photos land the paid gigs. And it stings.
> But in the next 30 days…
> You can learn to take the kind of shots clients happily pay for — without a decade of practice or fancy gear.
> Then, you can learn to stand out in a market full of iPhone "photographers" — without slashing your prices.
> And soon, you've got a camera that actually pays you.
> And you can do this even if you feel like just an amateur. Even if everyone swears the market's too saturated.
> That's because you can follow one portfolio-first method — using a repeatable shot-framework and a stand-out portfolio — the same simple system others have already used to land their first paying clients.
