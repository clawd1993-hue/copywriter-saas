# VSL Section 8 SOP — Product Overview

> **When to load:** VSL Section 8 (after Proof).
> **What it does:** the "here's the new opportunity" beat — name the vehicle, position it against the old failed way, fold in the 3 belief-breaks (outcome-framed), and hand off into the pitch.
> **Output:** a lean positioning + 3 belief-breaks + hand-off, pushed to the "Product Overview" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 6 Vehicle ONLY (name + positioning + the 3 secrets).

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 8: Product Overview. Proof showed them it works. This is where we name the new opportunity and make them BELIEVE it's real and for them — the vehicle, why it beats the old way, and the three belief-breaks that melt their 'yeah but' doubts. Then we hand straight into what they get."* Then write it.

## What it is
The "here's the new opportunity" beat. Name the vehicle, position it against the old failed way, and fold in the 3 belief-breaks — all **outcome-framed**. This is where they BELIEVE the opportunity is real and for them. It hands off directly into Section 9 (the deliverable reveal).

## Gather from Step 6 (copy straight from your Vehicle — the FULL secret, every piece)
- **Vehicle name** ← Part A name
- **Positioning** ← Part A ("the first [category] for [audience]; unlike [old ways ← Failures], it [superpower]")
- **🚗 belief-break** ← 🚗 secret — dream + "nothing works" + vehicle
- **🧠 belief-break** ← 🧠 secret — outcome + without (internal failure) + even-if + mechanism
- **🌍 belief-break** ← 🌍 secret — outcome + without (external failure) + even-if + mechanism

## The fill-in (a scaffold to say naturally — NOT a feature list)
> **[Name + positioning ← Part A]** "So I created **[Vehicle name]** — **[what it is in one line]**. Unlike **[the old ways ← Failures]**, it **[superpower]**.
> **[🚗 ← 🚗 secret]** Inside, you'll learn to **[🚗 dream]** — even if **[you've tried everything and 'nothing works']** — with **[the vehicle / simple system]**.
> **[🧠 ← 🧠 secret]** You'll learn to **[🧠 outcome]** — without **[🧠 internal failure]** — even if **[🧠 objection]** — with **[🧠 mechanism]**.
> **[🌍 ← 🌍 secret]** And you'll learn to **[🌍 outcome]** — without **[🌍 external failure]** — even if **[🌍 objection]** — with **[🌍 mechanism]**.
> **[Hand-off]** And here's exactly what you get to make that happen…"

> ⚠️ **Each belief-break carries its own failure — that's what makes it land.** 🚗 = the **"nothing works"** belief (= the SUM of the 🧠 internal + 🌍 external failures) · 🧠 = the **internal** failure (what they tried to fix themselves) · 🌍 = the **external** failure (what they tried to beat the odds). This **reinforces** Section 5's moves (same withouts) — so **phrase it differently here**, don't copy Section 5 word-for-word. And don't re-use the exact failure already named in the positioning line — pick the specific internal/external one.

Also read **the VSL so far** — flow out of Proof, and phrase the belief-breaks differently from Section 5's moves (don't repeat those lines).

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
The Product Overview card is **editable**, so DON'T ask permission — write it and push straight to the card. *(Overrides the usual "always ask" rule.)*
1. Open with the framing above (a line or two).
2. Write the positioning + 3 belief-breaks + the hand-off line.
3. **Immediately PUSH it** wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>`. Short confirmation FIRST (visible), then the markers.
4. Visible line — hand them the fork: *"Done — your Product Overview's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 9 (the Pitch — where we reveal exactly what they get)?"*

### Push content format (renders clean on the card)
```
[Name + positioning line]

[🚗 belief-break]
[🧠 belief-break]
[🌍 belief-break]

[Hand-off: "And here's exactly what you get to make that happen…"]
```

## Rules
- **Lean and clean** — one tight beat, not a feature list.
- This is **NOT the value stack and NOT the deliverable reveal** — that's Section 9.
- Keep the 3 belief-breaks **outcome-framed** ("you'll learn to…"), NOT product-framed ("you get the module") — save "you get" language for Section 9.
- These belief-breaks are the **MACRO** promise (why this opportunity works); Section 9's deliverable even-ifs are the **MICRO** proof. Don't write them as verbatim copies of the same objection back-to-back.
- **Use the vehicle name.**
- **End by handing off** into the deliverable reveal.
- 3rd-grade words.

## Quality test
Can someone read this and (a) know what the opportunity IS, and (b) believe it could work for them? If they don't believe yet — sharpen the positioning and belief-breaks.
