# VSL Section 3 SOP — Why (The Desire)

> **When to load:** VSL Section 3 (after the Shocking Statement).
> **What it does:** paint the dream — the magic-wand version of their life, made so real they FEEL it.
> **Output:** a 2–3 paragraph scene, pushed to the "Why (Desire)" card.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 3: Why — the Desire. We just jolted them awake. Now we make them WANT it. This is the magic-wand version of their life — so vivid they can see it, feel it, almost taste it. Not a list of benefits — a scene they star in."* Then write it.

## What it is
Paint the dream. The magic-wand version of their life, made so real they FEEL it. This is a **scene**, not a benefit list.

## Grounding (pull from Step 3 Research — never invent)
Write it as a **4-beat scene** (each beat says where it comes from):
1. 🌅 **The ordinary moment** ← Step 3 **Time Dimensions (Present)** — open on a real slice of their normal day. → *"Imagine waking up, pouring your coffee, checking your phone…"*
2. 🎯 **The win they SEE** ← Step 3 **Desires** — the #1 desire, made visual and concrete. → *"…three Stripe notifications from people you've never spoken to."*
3. 🕊️ **The freedom** ← Step 3 **Pains** *(flipped to "no more…")* — the grind they're now free of. → *"No posting, no DMs, no calls with tire-kickers."*
4. 🏆 **The peak** *(optional, but the strongest ending)* ← Step 3 **Motivations + secret Desires** — the identity / emotional payoff. **End here.** → *"You're a real CEO, not a job. Your income is yours, not the algorithm's."*

Also read **the VSL so far** (Hook + Shocking Statement) so this flows from them and doesn't repeat a line.

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
The Why (Desire) card is **editable**, so DON'T hold it in the chat for approval and DON'T ask permission to push. *(Overrides the usual "always ask" rule.)*
1. Open with the framing above (a line or two).
2. Write the scene — 2–3 short paragraphs, present tense, sensory, ending on the emotional peak.
3. **Immediately PUSH it** to the card, wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>`. Short confirmation FIRST (visible), then the markers.
4. Visible line: **don't re-print it, don't ask permission** — hand them the fork: *"Done — your Desire scene's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 4 (Why — the Pain)?"*

### Push content format (renders clean on the card)
```
[The 2–3 paragraph scene — present tense, ending on the emotional peak.]
```

## Rules
- **Present tense** ("Imagine waking up and…").
- **Sensory** — what do they SEE, HEAR, FEEL?
- Identity / relationship payoffs, not just practical wins.
- 2–3 short paragraphs. Generous white space.
- **End on the emotional peak** — the thing they want MOST.
- A scene, never a bullet list of benefits.
- 3rd-grade words; use the avatar's own language from the research.

## Quality test
Does it make the avatar tear up or get chills? If it reads like a bullet-point list of benefits — rewrite it as a scene.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Opens on a real present-day moment (← Time Dimensions).
- [ ] Shows the #1 desire, concrete and visual (← Desires).
- [ ] Names the freedom from the grind (← Pains, flipped).
- [ ] Ends on the identity/emotional peak (← Motivations).
- [ ] Present tense, sensory, 2–3 short paragraphs, 3rd-grade level.
- [ ] Reads as a scene, not a benefit list; flows from the prior sections.
