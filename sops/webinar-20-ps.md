# Webinar Section 20 SOP — P.S. (the final flash)

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 20 — the LAST section (right after the Future-Pace Close).
> **What it does:** the last thing they read/hear — restate the dream + the method (compressed) + the urgency. Must STAND ALONE (skimmers jump straight here).
> **Output:** the P.S., pushed to the "P.S." card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 3 (the dream) + Step 7 (Method = the 3 moves, outcomes only) + the urgency from §16.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 20: the P.S. — the last thing they read, and it has to stand on its own because skimmers jump straight here. Max 3 sentences: the dream, how simple the path is, and the reason to act now."* Then write it.

## How to write it (max 3 sentences)
1. **The dream** ← Step 3 — where they end up.
2. **How simple the path is** — the **3 moves in ONE line** ← Step 7 (outcomes only, no mechanisms).
3. **Urgency / consequence** ← the §16 urgency + point at the button.

## Spoken example (match this voice)
> "P.S. — Remember, a booked-out calendar at rates you're proud of is closer than you think, and all it takes is three simple moves: [move 1], [move 2], [move 3]. But those [20] spots close soon — click the button above before they're gone."

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push. Don't reprint in chat. Solo.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- This is the FINAL section — after it pushes, tell them the whole VSL is done.
- Visible line: *"Done — your P.S. is in the card. 🎉 That's all 20 sections — your webinar VSL is complete. Read it top to bottom, tweak anything, and you're ready to record."*

## Rules
- **Max 3 sentences.** Must stand alone.
- Restate the dream (← Step 3) AND the method (3 moves compressed ← Step 7, outcomes only).
- End on urgency / emotional consequence (← §16) + point at the button.
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
If they ONLY saw the P.S. — would they still want in?

## Quality gate
- [ ] Max 3 sentences; stands alone.
- [ ] Dream (Step 3) + 3 moves in one line (Step 7, outcomes only) + §16 urgency.
- [ ] Ends on urgency/consequence + points at the button; spoken tone, 3rd-grade.
