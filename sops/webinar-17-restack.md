# Webinar Section 17 SOP — Re-Stack (the Stack Slide)

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 17 (right after Guarantee & Urgency, right BEFORE the CTA).
> **What it does:** one fast run-through of the ENTIRE offer — names + $values only — summed to a total that dwarfs the price. The final flash before the button.
> **Output:** the re-stack, pushed to the "Re-Stack" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 8 (the full stack) = the SAME items already revealed in §11 (core/pitch) + §12 (bonuses). **Recap them — invent NOTHING new.**

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 17: the Re-Stack. This is a fast recap of everything they get — core + every bonus + values — summed to the total, right before the button. Recap, don't re-explain. Momentum, not new info."* Then write it.

## How to write it (recap, don't re-explain)
One tight run-through — **names + $values only**, no re-explaining what each thing does:
1. **Core deliverables** ← Step 8 / §11 — name each + its $value.
2. **Every bonus** ← Step 8 / §12 — name each + its $value.
3. **Sum to the TOTAL value** → *"That's $[total] in real value…"*
4. **Anchor against the price** → *"…and today you get all of it for just $[price]."*
5. Straight into the CTA.

## If any $values are still TBD
Ask the user to set them (or confirm they're already in Step 8) so the total actually lands — a re-stack with missing numbers doesn't hit. Otherwise pull straight from Step 8 / §11 / §12.

## Spoken example (match this voice)
> "So let's recap everything you get the second you join. You get [Core Program] — that's $[X]. You get [Bonus 1] — $[X]. [Bonus 2] — $[X]. [Bonus 3] — $[X]. Add it all up, that's **$[total]** in real value. And today? You're getting all of it for just **$[price]**."

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push. Don't reprint in chat. Solo — no ask unless values are missing.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- End handing into the CTA: *"So here's exactly what to do…"*
- Visible line: *"Done — your Re-Stack's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 18 (the CTA)?"*

## Rules
- **Recap, don't re-explain** — names + $values only, momentum not new info.
- **Invent nothing new** — every item already appeared in §11/§12 (← Step 8).
- **Sum to a total that dwarfs the price** (~10x).
- Hand into the CTA.
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Can they feel the whole value pile in ~20 seconds, and does the total obviously dwarf the price?

## Quality gate
- [ ] Recaps core + every bonus (names + $values only), all from Step 8 / §11-12 — nothing new invented.
- [ ] Sums to a total that dwarfs the price (~10x).
- [ ] Recap not re-explain; momentum; hands into the CTA.
