# VSL Section 1 SOP — The Hook

> **When to load:** VSL Section 1, the FIRST section of the sales letter (after the 8-step offer engine is complete).
> **What it does:** write the opening line of the VSL — the one that stops the scroll and makes them think *"wait… how?"*
> **Output:** one Hook line (two sentences max), pushed to the "Hook" card.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
This is a new chapter — we're done building the offer, now we WRITE the VSL. Frame it: *"Now Section 1: the Hook. This is the very first line of your video — the one job here is to stop the scroll and make a cold stranger think 'wait… how?' in one breath. Everything we built feeds it."* Then draft.

## What the Hook is
The first line. It stops the scroll — **promises the dream, kills the pain, names the new way**, all in one breath, so they think *"wait… how?"*

## Grounding (pull from the board — never invent)
- **[DREAM]** ← Step 3 Research → **Desires** — the #1 thing they want bad enough to click.
- **[PAIN]** ← Step 3 Research → **Pains** — the thing they most want to stop doing.
- **[OBJECTION]** ← Step 3 Research → **Objections** — the "yeah, but not for ME" belief. *This is the even-if — it's what keeps them watching instead of clicking away.*
- **[VEHICLE]** ← Step 6 Vehicle — the new way / one concept (use the positioning, not the brand name unless it helps).

## The formula (a scaffold, not a template to read aloud)
> "How to get **[DREAM]** without **[PAIN]** — even if **[OBJECTION]** — using **[VEHICLE]**."

Write it so it reads like a human said it, not like blanks got filled in. The formula is the skeleton; the words are the avatar's own (from the research).

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
The Hook card on the dashboard is **editable**, so DON'T hold options in the chat waiting for approval, and DON'T ask permission to push. Write the single strongest Hook and push it **straight to the card** — they tweak it there, not in the chat. *(This overrides the usual "always ask before pushing" rule.)*
1. Open with the framing above (a line or two).
2. Pull the 4 pieces from the board and write the ONE best Hook (in-formula, one even-if, 3rd-grade).
3. **Immediately PUSH it** to the Hook card, wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>`. Short confirmation FIRST (visible), then the markers.
4. In the visible line: **don't re-print the Hook and don't ask permission** — just hand them the fork: *"Done — your Hook's in the card. Tweak it right there if you want, or ready for Section 2 (the Shocking Statement)?"*

*(Only if they ASK for alternatives: give 2–3 in chat and push their pick. Default = push the one best Hook, let them edit it in the card.)*

### Push content format (renders clean on the card)
```
[The final Hook line — one sentence, two max.]
```
(Just the Hook itself — no headers, no "Option 1." The card shows the locked line.)

## Rules
- ONE sentence, two max. **3rd-grade words.**
- Must break a belief they hold ("I can't get X without Y").
- Must name the vehicle / new way.
- **ONE even-if, not two** — two muddies it.
- Specific beats clever — *"Make $5k/month selling faceless digital products"* beats *"Unlock your online income potential."*
- Use the avatar's exact language from the research — not marketer-speak.

## Quality test
Does a cold stranger stop and think *"wait, how?"* If it sounds like every other ad they've scrolled past — rewrite.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Dream, pain, objection all pulled from the REAL Step 3 research (their words).
- [ ] Vehicle / new way named (from Step 6).
- [ ] Exactly ONE even-if.
- [ ] One sentence (two max), 3rd-grade reading level.
- [ ] Breaks a real belief; specific, not clever-vague.
- [ ] Passes the "wait… how?" test.
