# Call-Booker Section 14 SOP — P.S. (nudge to BOOK)

> **When to load:** Call-Booker VSL Section 14 (the FINAL section). Same as the DTS P.S. — but it nudges them to BOOK, not buy.
> **What it does:** the last thing they read — restate the dream + the method + a final nudge to book the free call.
> **Output:** a P.S. (max 3 sentences), pushed to the "P.S." card. Completes the call-booker VSL.
> **Pulls from:** Step 3 Desires + Step 7 Method.

## Opening framing
*"Final one — the P.S. Skimmers jump straight here, so it has to stand alone: the dream, the dead-simple how, and one last nudge to BOOK the free call (not buy)."* Then write it.

## How to write it (same as DTS P.S. — but nudge to BOOK)
1. **#1 desire** ← Step 3 — the thing they want most.
2. **Method compressed** ← Step 7 — the 3 steps in one sentence.
3. **Nudge to BOOK** — the free call, limited spots, no cost to find out. NOT "buy for $X."

## The formula
> "P.S. — Remember, **[dream outcome]** is **[timeframe]** away. All you do is **[method compressed]**. **[Book the free call — limited spots / nothing to lose.]**"

## How to run it (write → push → celebrate the finish)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat. This is the LAST section.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- Visible line celebrates the whole VSL done: *"🎉 That's it — your full call-booker VSL is done, all 14 sections on the board. Scroll it top to bottom; it reads as one letter now. Nice work."*

## Rules
- Max 3 sentences; restate the dream AND the method.
- End on the **book-the-call** nudge (never a price).
- Reuse the real urgency (limited slots) — don't invent.
- 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
If someone ONLY read the P.S. — would they book the call? If not, rewrite until it stands alone.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Restates the #1 dream (← Step 3) + method compressed (← Step 7).
- [ ] Ends on a book-the-free-call nudge (no price).
- [ ] Max 3 sentences, 3rd-grade, stands alone; closing chat celebrates the finished VSL.
