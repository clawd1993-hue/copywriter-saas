# Webinar Section 9 SOP — Transition to the Offer (permission to sell + the two paths)

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 9 (right after Secret #3 — the hinge from teaching into selling).
> **What it does:** the pivot from FREE teaching → the offer. You just gave the whole method — now (a) land that they HAVE it, (b) show the two paths, (c) **ask permission** to pitch, (d) hand into Product Overview. Breaks the "here comes the sale" tension so the pitch feels invited.
> **Output:** a short permission-to-sell hinge, pushed to the "Transition to the Offer" card.
> **Pulls from:** Brunson technique (mostly outside the system). Light pulls: the **method just taught** (§5-8) = the DIY path · the **product** (Step 8.1) = the shortcut path · the **dream** (Step 7) to re-anchor.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 9: the Transition. This is the hinge — we've taught the whole method for free, now we ask permission to show them the shortcut. Land that they already have it, lay out the two paths, and get a yes before we pitch."* Then write it.

## The 4 beats (short — this is a hinge, not a section)
1. **Land the method** ← §5-8: *"So that's the whole thing — all 3 secrets, the entire method. You could honestly walk out right now and go do this."*
2. **The two paths** — DIY (the method ← §5-8) **vs** shortcut (the product ← Step 8.1):
   *"From here you've got two options. **Path 1:** go do all of it yourself, by hand — 100% doable, just takes [time/effort]. **Path 2:** let me hand you the shortcut that does it for you."*
   → **Make Path 1 CONCRETE** — name the actual steps you just taught + point to what you demoed ("just like you watched me do"). Vague DIY = weak fork. A real, honest DIY path is what makes Path 2 the obvious pick (not a hard sell).
3. **Permission to sell** *(Brunson — the psychological yes):* *"Quick question before I go on — is it cool if I take a few minutes to show you exactly what I built to help you do this the fast way?"* → tension-break, wait for the yes.
4. **Hand into the offer** → Product Overview.

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat. Solo.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- Visible line: *"Done — your Transition's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 10 (Product Overview)?"*

## Rules
- **ALWAYS ask permission** (the yes before the pitch).
- Make **Path 1 (DIY) genuinely real + concrete** (you just taught it — it's honest) → Path 2 becomes the obvious shortcut.
- **Short** — a hinge. **No price / no stack here** (that's §10 → §14).
- Reference the shortcut as *"what I built"* / *"the shortcut"* — **save the product-name reveal for §10** (Product Overview).
- ⚠️ **"Two paths" ≠ "two choices."** This fork = DIY vs shortcut (pre-pitch). The *"leave and nothing changes / or join"* fork is the CTA close (§18-19) — keep them separate.
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Does the pitch now feel **invited** (they said yes), and does Path 2 read as the obvious shortcut rather than a hard sell? If Path 1 is vague, it doesn't work — make it concrete.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Lands the method (they HAVE it, ← §5-8).
- [ ] Two paths: concrete DIY vs the shortcut (no price/stack).
- [ ] Asks permission to pitch (waits for the yes).
- [ ] Product-name reveal held for §10; "two paths" not confused with the CTA fork.
- [ ] Short, spoken, 3rd-grade; hands into Product Overview.
