# Webinar Section 3 SOP — Future Pace

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 3 (after the Origin Story).
> **What it does:** Brunson's intro future pace — drop them into the **after-world** and let them FEEL the dream, EARLY, before any teaching. ONE job: **make them want it.** Opens the desire loop the whole webinar then pays off.
> **Output:** a present-tense sensory dream scene ending on a trial close, pushed to the "Future Pace" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 3 (Desires & Motivations). NO interview — pull the dream straight from the research. **No method/vehicle here** (that's §4 + the secrets).

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 3: Future Pace. Before we teach a single thing, we make them FEEL the dream — drop them into the life they want, so vividly they can taste it. This isn't 'here's how' — it's 'imagine actually living it.' That craving is what makes them lean into the secrets to learn the how."* Then write it.

## Don't blur it with its two cousins
- **§1 Hook** = the *claim* ("I'll show you how to…").
- **§3 Future Pace (this)** = the *feeling* ("imagine actually living it") → desire → they lean into the secrets.
- **§19 Future-Pace Close** = the *payoff of buying* ("90 days after you click today…").

## How to write it
- **Present tense, sensory** — a scene they can SEE themselves inside.
- Pull the **REAL** desire from Step 3 — not just the surface win ("more sales / more bookings") but the **deeper one underneath**: freedom, status, *"I'm finally the person who cracked this."*
- **Vivid > long.**
- **End on a TRIAL CLOSE** — a soft yes-question that gets a small internal nod.

## Spoken example (match this voice)
> "Imagine [sensory dream scene ← Step 3]… no more [pain]… just [the deeper payoff]. *(trial close)* If you could have that — wouldn't it be worth [X] minutes with me today?"

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat. Solo — no interview.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- Visible line: *"Done — your Future Pace is in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 4 (Set the Frame)?"*

## Rules
- **Present tense.** Make them **FEEL** it — don't list benefits.
- Pull the **deep / secret desire**, not the obvious one.
- **No "how," no vehicle, no webinar logistics** — pure feeling.
- **End on a trial close.**
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Do they feel a pull — *"I want that"* — and lean in? If it reads like a benefits list instead of a scene they're living, rewrite it.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Present-tense sensory scene (← Step 3 deep desire, not the surface win).
- [ ] Makes them FEEL it; no how/vehicle/logistics.
- [ ] Ends on a trial close.
- [ ] Distinct from the Hook (feeling, not claim); hands into Set the Frame.
- [ ] Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.
