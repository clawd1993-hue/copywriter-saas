# Webinar Section 19 SOP — Future-Pace Close

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 19 (right after the CTA).
> **What it does:** the forward vision AFTER the offer — put them in the after-world so buying = choosing their future self.
> **Output:** the future-pace close, pushed to the "Future-Pace Close" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 1 / Step 3 (the dream / post-solution life). Picks up from §18's hand-in ("So picture this…").

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 19: the Future-Pace Close. This paints their life AFTER they say yes — a present-tense day-in-the-life a bit down the road — so buying feels like choosing their future self. It picks up right where the CTA left off."* Then write it.

## Picks up from §18
The CTA handed in with *"So picture this…"* — you're painting the reward for the click they were just asked to make. Open right there.

## How to write it
- **Present-tense, sensory** — a **day-in-the-life ~[timeframe] out** (pick a believable horizon — 90 days, 6 months, a year).
- Same dream as Step 3, but framed as **the payoff of THIS decision** (using [Vehicle]) — **don't repeat Step 3 verbatim**, show it as lived scenes.
- Concrete details they can feel (the calendar, the message that comes in, the number in the account, the way they feel walking around).
- **Land on:** *that future starts with one click today* — point back at the button.

## Spoken example (match this voice)
> "So picture this. It's six months from now. You wake up, check your phone, and there are two new booking requests waiting — at your new rates. Your calendar for the month is already half full. That camera that used to sit in a bag? It's paying your rent now. You're not hoping anymore, you're booked. …That version of you? It starts with one click. Today."

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push. Don't reprint in chat. Solo.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- End handing into the P.S.: *"Oh — one more thing before you go…"*
- Visible line: *"Done — your Future-Pace Close is in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 20 (the P.S.) — the final one?"*

## Rules
- **Present-tense, sensory day-in-the-life** ~[timeframe] out.
- The dream (← Step 1/3) framed as the payoff of THIS click — **don't repeat Step 3 verbatim.**
- Acting = moving toward the life they want; not acting = staying stuck.
- **End pointing back at the button** (*starts with one click today*).
- Hand into the P.S.
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Can they *feel* themselves living the after-world, and does it end pulling them back to the button?
