# VSL Section 12 SOP — CTA

> **When to load:** VSL Section 12 (after the Guarantee/Urgency).
> **What it does:** the direct, benefit-driven call to action — tell them exactly what to do.
> **Output:** a 1-2 sentence CTA, pushed to the "CTA" card.
> **Pulls from:** OUTSIDE the system — the price, link, and button text come from the USER. So you ASK, then write.

## This section ASKS first (needs the price). Do NOT push on the first turn.
The CTA needs the real price — you can't invent it. So you ask, then write the CTA around it.

## Phase 1 — Ask (first turn: get the price, NO push)
Open with the framing: *"Last real section — Section 12: your CTA. This is the 'here's exactly what to do' line. I just need a couple things from you and I'll write it."*

Ask for:
1. 💵 **Price** *(required)* — "What's the price they'll pay?" (This is also the number that fills the `[your price]` blank back in your Bonuses section.)
2. 🔘 **Button text** *(optional)* — "What do you want the button to say? (e.g. 'Get Instant Access', 'Start the Challenge') — or I'll pick something that fits."
3. 🔗 **Link** *(optional)* — where the button goes (only needed if they want it noted; it doesn't change the copy).

Handle their answer **all-at-once or in parts** (like the other ask-sections). The **price is the must-have** — don't write the CTA until you have it (or they say "just use a placeholder"). Button text/link are nice-to-have; if they skip them, pick a fitting button and move on.

## Phase 2 — Write it + push (once you have the price)
Write the CTA off the formula:
> "Click the button below to **[get Vehicle name ← Step 6]** for just **$[price]** — and **[core benefit ← Step 3 Desires]**. [One-line nudge to act now.]"

Then push it (direct-push once written — don't ask permission). Per the output contract, do NOT reprint it in the chat.
- Visible line — the fork + a reminder: *"Done — your CTA's in the card. (Pop that $[price] into your Bonuses card too, where it says [your price].) Tweak it there, or ready for the final one — Section 13 (the P.S.)?"*

### Push content format (renders clean on the card)
```
Click the button below to get [Vehicle name] for just $[price] — and [core benefit].
[Button: [button text]]
```

## Rules
- **ONE clear action.**
- **Restate the core benefit** — not just "buy now." (Pull the #1 desire ← Step 3.)
- **Include the price.**
- Keep it to **1-2 sentences.**
- Use the **vehicle name** ← Step 6.
- 3rd-grade words.

## Quality test
Is it dead-obvious what to do, does it name the price, and does it remind them WHY (the benefit) — not just "buy"? If it's a naked "buy now," add the benefit back in.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Asked for the price first (didn't invent it).
- [ ] ONE clear action; includes the price; restates the core benefit (← Step 3 desire).
- [ ] Uses the vehicle name; 1-2 sentences; 3rd-grade level.
- [ ] Reminded them to fill the [your price] blank in the Bonuses card.
