# Call-Booker Section 13 SOP — CTA (BOOK the call, not buy)

> **When to load:** Call-Booker VSL Section 13. The CTA books a call — it never sells a price.
> **What it does:** tell them exactly what to do — book the free call.
> **Output:** a 1–2 sentence book-the-call CTA, pushed to the "CTA" card.
> **Pulls from:** Vehicle name (Step 6) + core benefit (Step 3 desire). NO price — the button books a call.

## Opening framing
*"Now Section 13: the CTA. On a call-booker this books a call — it doesn't sell a price. 'Click below to book your free call' — restate the benefit, one clear action. I just need your booking link + button text."* Then write it.

## This ASKS first (needs the booking link). Do NOT push on the first turn.
Ask for:
1. 🔗 **Booking link / calendar** *(the must-have — where the button sends them to book)*
2. 🔘 **Button text** *(optional — e.g. "Book My Free Call" / "Grab Your Spot" — or I'll pick one)*

Handle answers all-at-once or in parts. Don't write until you have the link (or they say "use a placeholder").

## Phase 2 — write it + push (once you have the link)
Write the CTA off this formula:
> "Click the button below to **book your free [call/strategy session]** — and **[core benefit ← Step 3 Desires]**. [One-line nudge — spots are limited / no cost to find out.]"

Then direct-push (don't ask permission). Don't reprint in chat.
- Visible line: *"Done — your CTA's in the card (books the free call, no price). Tweak it there, or ready for the last one — Section 14 (the P.S.)?"*

### Push content format
```
Click the button below to book your free [call] — and [core benefit].
[Button: [button text]]
```

## Rules
- **BOOK the call, not buy.** No price, ever.
- ONE clear action; restate the core benefit (← Step 3); use the vehicle name where it fits.
- 1–2 sentences. 3rd-grade.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Asked for the booking link first (didn't invent it).
- [ ] Books a free call — no price, one clear action, restates the benefit.
- [ ] 1–2 sentences, 3rd-grade; flows from the urgency line.
