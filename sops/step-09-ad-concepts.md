# Step 9 SOP — Ad Concepts (the assembly line)

> **When to load:** Step 9 (the last step of the offer engine), after Steps 1–8.
> **What it does:** turn everything upstream into modular ad "bricks" — **Hooks × Meat × CTAs** — that combine into ~60 ready ads.
> **Output:** 2 awareness buckets + 10 hooks + 2 meats + 3 CTAs + the 60-ad matrix. This is the "GOATed Ads assembly line."

## The big idea (frame it for the user)
Treat ads like **Lego bricks**: interchangeable Hooks, Meat (the body), and CTAs. You only *write* ~15 pieces, but mixing & matching spits out **60 ads** — so you never wonder "how do I get enough creatives?" again. Hooks do 80% of the work; more variations = more winners, faster.

## Grounding (pull from the board — never invent)
- **Hooks / pains** ← Step 3 (pains, objections) for problem-aware; Step 6 (Big Promise, secrets) for solution-aware.
- **Meat / mechanism** ← Step 6 (Vehicle) + Step 7 (Method) + Step 8 (offer).
- **CTA target** ← the user's offer / entry point (from Step 8). If the exact offer name/price/link isn't clear, keep the CTA generic and ask them to drop it in — never invent a price.
- **Proof** ← honest soft proof only. NEVER fabricate numbers/case studies.

## How to run it (propose the full kit → review → push)
Open with the standard step framing, then generate the whole kit and present it for review. One clarifier max: if you don't know what they want the ad to send people to (the offer/link/price), ask that one thing before writing the CTAs.

### 1) The 2 awareness buckets (1–2 lines each)
- **Problem-aware** — they feel the pain but don't know the fix. Pain-driven. (e.g. "my marketing isn't working.")
- **Solution-aware** — they know a solution exists, they want the best one. Promise-driven.
Anchor both to the real market/research.

### 2) The 10 hooks (5 + 5) — standalone 3–5 second lines
Use varied **hook types**: labels · questions · conditionals ("if you…") · provocative statements. The hook is 80% of the ad.
- **5 Problem-aware hooks (pain-driven)** ← Step 3 pains/objections. *(e.g. "If your VSL isn't turning strangers into calls, you're burning money.")*
- **5 Solution-aware hooks (promise-driven)** ← Step 6 Big Promise/secrets. *(e.g. "The fastest way to turn cold traffic into booked calls with one VSL.")*

### 3) The 2 meats (bodies, ~30–45s each) — reused with every hook
- **Meat A — Problem-aware story:** expand the pain → a brief story/example of someone stuck there → introduce your mechanism (Vehicle/Method) → one proof point → tease the outcome ("that's how they went from X to Y").
- **Meat B — Solution-aware proof/promise:** restate the Big Promise → show how your mechanism is *different* from the DIY / generic way → a quick case/result → tease how they can see it or join.

### 4) The 3 CTAs — near-identical, tiny wording tweaks, 3–5s each
Point to the user's offer/next step. *(e.g. "Click the link and grab [offer] on the next page." / "Tap 'Learn More' to get [offer] now." / "Hit the link and start [offer] today.")*

### 5) The 60-ad matrix (explain the payoff)
Each of the 10 hooks pairs with **Meat A or B** × **CTA 1/2/3** = **6 combos per hook × 10 hooks = 60 ads.** One shoot, one editor pass, 60 creatives.

## Delivering + push
1. Present the full kit in chat (buckets → 10 hooks → 2 meats → 3 CTAs → the matrix). Keep it scannable.
2. Invite tweaks: *"Want to swap any hooks, tighten a meat, or change the CTA target? Otherwise I'll lock the whole ad kit to your board."*
3. On their go, PUSH the kit wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation FIRST — this is the LAST step, so celebrate + point to the copy, not "Step 10").

## ✅ Step 9 is DONE when
The full ad kit is on the "Ad Concepts" card. This is the last step of the engine, so close with a win AND hand them forward into the VSL — end on this exact invitation:
> "🎉 That's your full offer engine complete — all 8 cards on your board: desire, market, research, problems & solutions, vehicle, method, offer stack, and now 60 ads ready to film. Everything downstream is built on this. **Ready to start your VSL at Section 1?**"

## Quality gate
- [ ] 2 buckets defined; 10 hooks (5 pain ← S3 · 5 promise ← S6), varied hook types, each a standalone 3–5s line.
- [ ] 2 meats built from Vehicle/Method/Offer; honest soft proof (no fabricated numbers).
- [ ] 3 near-identical CTAs pointing at the real offer (asked for it if unknown).
- [ ] The 60-ad matrix explained.
