# Webinar Section 12 SOP — Bonuses

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 12 (right after the core Stack).
> **What it does:** stack the free gifts on top of the core — value piling even higher. Each traces to a specific Step 4 problem.
> **Output:** the bonus stack (each: Edwards bullet + value), pushed to the "Bonuses" card. **No price/total here** (that's §14).
> **Pulls from:** Step 8.2 — the curated bonus stack (NEVER raw Step 5) + the vehicle name.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 12: Bonuses. The core's stacked — now we pile the free gifts on top, each one crushing a leftover problem, value climbing higher. We still hold the price (that's a couple sections away) — this just makes the stack feel unbeatable."* Then write it.

## How to write it
- Frame as **"and when you join today, you also get…"**
- Reveal each bonus (from **Step 8.2**) with its **full Edwards bullet** (so you can… even if… which means…) + a **dollar value.**
- Each bonus traces to a real Step 4 problem — believable-but-impressive values.
- **No price, no grand total here** — value just keeps piling. (§14 does the anchor + price.)

## Spoken example (match this voice)
> "And when you join today, you also get [Bonus] — so you can [outcome], even if [objection], which means [payoff]. On its own that's worth $[X]. You also get [Bonus]…"

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat. Solo.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- End the copy handing into Trial Closes: *"So before I show you the price, let me ask you a few quick questions…"*
- Visible line: *"Done — your Bonuses are in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 13 (Trial Closes)?"*

## Rules
- Use the **packaged Step 8.2 bonuses** (never raw Step 5).
- Each = full Edwards bullet + a believable-but-impressive value.
- Frame as **free gifts for joining today.**
- **No price / no total** (that's §14).
- Hand into Trial Closes.
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Do the bonuses **ALONE** feel worth more than the price they're about to hear? If they feel like filler, pick different ones (from 8.2).
