# Call-Booker Section 9 SOP — The Pitch (HOLD THE PRICE)

> **When to load:** Call-Booker VSL Section 9. Same as the DTS Pitch — EXCEPT the price is never on the page.
> **What it does:** reveal everything they get + the built-in even-ifs, so desire peaks — but the price + close happen ON THE CALL.
> **Pulls from:** Step 8.1 (the core: flagship roadmap + 🧠/🌍 components).

## Opening framing
*"Now the Pitch. This is where we show them everything they get — but on a call-booker we never put a price on the page. We reveal the goods, peak the desire, and let the price + close happen on the call."* Then write it.

## Reveal it the same as the DTS Pitch — with ONE change: hold the price
- Reveal the **flagship as a tangible roadmap** (Deliverable #1, in the lead-in — a concrete thing they get, not a vague "you'll finally…"), then **"And you'll get…"** → the **🧠/🌍 component bullets** (name → so you can → even if → which means), mirrored from Step 8.1.
- **HOLD THE PRICE. Never state or imply a total or a price.**
- **Dollar values on the pieces are OPTIONAL** — keep them only if they DON'T telegraph the price. If listing values would hint at the price (or beg the "so what's it cost?" question awkwardly), **drop the values entirely** — the goods sell themselves; the number comes on the call.
- **No value-vs-price math** (that's the call's job — this isn't the DTS stack close).

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat.
1. Open with the framing.
2. Reveal flagship roadmap → "And you'll get…" → the 🧠/🌍 bullets (values optional, price held).
3. PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
4. Visible line: *"Done — your Pitch's in the card (price held for the call). Tweak it there, or ready for Section 10 (the Bonuses)?"*

## Rules
- Same reveal shape as DTS: flagship roadmap (Deliverable #1) + 🧠/🌍 bullets, Edwards formula.
- **NEVER show the price or a grand total.** Values on pieces optional; drop them if they telegraph price.
- Conversational, 3rd-grade.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Flagship = tangible roadmap in the lead-in; "And you'll get…" → 🧠/🌍 bullets from Step 8.1.
- [ ] No price, no total, no value-vs-price math anywhere.
- [ ] Values on pieces kept ONLY if they don't hint at price.
