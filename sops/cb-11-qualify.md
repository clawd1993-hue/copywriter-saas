# Call-Booker Section 11 SOP — Who This Is For (Qualify)

> **When to load:** Call-Booker VSL Section 11 (NEW — only exists on the call-booker). After the Bonuses.
> **What it does:** the filter. Desire is peaked (Pitch + Bonuses) — now say plainly who the call is FOR and who it's NOT for. Take-away selling: filtering raises desire.
> **Output:** a FOR / NOT-FOR filter that bridges into the CTA, pushed to the "Who This Is For" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 2 (Market — who the avatar is) + Step 3 (Desires + their real situation). Disqualifiers = real wrong-fit traits.

## Opening framing
*"Now Section 11: Who This Is For. Their desire is maxed right after the stack — this is where we qualify. Say plainly who the call is for and who it's not. Two jobs: protect your calendar from tire-kickers, AND make the right person raise their hand HARDER. Filtering IS selling."* Then write it.

## What it is
The filter. They've seen everything they get (Pitch) and every objection crushed (Bonuses) — desire is peaked. Now you say who the call is *for* and who it's *NOT* for. **Take-away selling** — people want what screens them in. It also frames the call as *exclusive*, not a desperate pitch.

## How to write it
1. **Open with the turn** — signal you're about to filter: *"This isn't for everyone, so let me be straight about who it's for."*
2. List **3–4 "FOR you if…"** ← Step 2 (who they are) + Step 3 (their situation/desire).
3. List **2–3 "NOT for you if…"** — real disqualifiers. Make **ONE the "won't do the work" filter** (screens tire-kickers AND makes doers lean in).
4. **Bridge into the CTA**: *"If that first list is you, here's your next step…"*

## The shape
```
This isn't for everyone, so let me be straight about who it's for.

This is for you if:
- [FOR criterion ← Step 2/3]
- [FOR criterion ← Step 2/3]
- [FOR criterion ← Step 3 desire]

This is NOT for you if:
- [disqualifier — wrong stage]
- [disqualifier — the "won't do the work" filter]

If that first list sounds like you, here's your next step…
```

## How to run it (write → push → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- Visible line: *"Done — your Qualify section's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 12 (Guarantee & Urgency)?"*

## Rules
- FOR list = Step 2 + Step 3, **not invented.**
- One NOT-for = the exact **"won't do the work"** filter.
- Tone = take-away ("I only want the right people"), **never desperation** — raises status AND desire.
- Keep it tight: **3–4 for, 2–3 not-for.** Not a giant checklist.
- End by bridging to the CTA.
- 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Would your *ideal* client read the "for you if" list and think *"that's literally me"*? Would a tire-kicker read "not for you if" and bow out? If it's so broad everyone qualifies — sharpen it. A filter that filters no one isn't a filter.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Opens on the take-away turn ("This isn't for everyone…").
- [ ] 3–4 FOR (← Step 2/3) + 2–3 NOT-FOR, one being the "won't do the work" filter.
- [ ] Take-away tone, not desperation; tight, not a checklist.
- [ ] Bridges into the CTA; nothing invented.
