# Step 8 SOP — Package the Deliverables (Value Stack)

> **When to load:** Step 8, after Steps 5, 6 & 7 are on the board.
> **What it does:** package the offer into a value stack — **8.1 Core (3, belief-mapped, auto)** then **8.2 Bonuses (chosen with the user)**.
> **Output:** the full offer stack. VSL project (the default). Everything is lifted from upstream — nothing new is invented.

## Grounding (pull from the board — never invent deliverables)
- **🚗 core** ← the **Vehicle** (Step 6 Part A) — the container / Big Promise.
- **🧠 / 🌍 core** ← the **🧠 / 🌍 mechanisms** from Step 6 (each is an already-named Step 5 solution).
- **Bonuses** ← the **rest** of the Step 5 solutions (the Step 4/5 map).
- **Payoffs ("which means")** ← Step 3 **Motivations**.
- **Perceived values** ← you *propose* an anchor (what it'd cost to solve WITHOUT them — not their price), and invite the user to adjust. Never present a value as a hard fact.

## Open the step (this exact 2-part framing) — then WAIT
When the user arrives at Step 8, your FIRST message is ONLY the framing below — do NOT build anything yet. Present the 2 parts, ask if they're ready, and stop.
> "Now Step 8: your Deliverables — this is where everything you've built becomes the actual **offer stack** people buy. We'll do it in 2 parts: first your **core deliverables** (the 3 that kill their biggest doubts), then your **free bonus stack** that fills in every remaining gap. Ready to start with **8.1 — the core**?"

Only after their go, run 8.1.

---

# 8.1 — THE 3 CORE DELIVERABLES  (auto-build, auto-push — then lead into bonuses)

The core covers all 3 beliefs: **🚗 = the Big Promise (lead-in / container), 🧠 + 🌍 = the 2 component bullets.** Pull the existing names — do NOT re-describe.

**Format (Edwards bullet):**
- **🚗 lead-in + flagship value:** "When you join **[Vehicle name ← Part A]**, you'll finally **[dream ← 🚗]** — without **[failures ← 🚗]** — even if **[nothing's worked ← 🚗]**. Here's everything inside…"  The flagship (the main program/system) carries the **biggest** value in the stack — give it a believable big anchor: **[Vehicle name] *(Value: $X — biggest anchor)***.
- **🧠 + 🌍 bullets:** "Inside, you'll **[get / learn]** **[Name ← the mechanism]** so you can **[outcome ← belief]** — even if **[objection ← belief]** — which means **[payoff ← Step 3 Motivations]**. *(Value $X)*"  *("get" = tool/kit · "learn" = skill/method.)*

**Coverage check:** all 3 beliefs covered (🚗 lead-in + 🧠 + 🌍). If one isn't → flag the hole.

## Delivering 8.1 — DON'T ask to push, just do it
The core pulls entirely from stuff already defined (Step 6/5), so there's nothing to approve — **build it and AUTO-PUSH it** (a PENDING push). This OVERRIDES core-brain's "always ask before pushing" — for the 8.1 core only. Then in the **same message**, jump straight into the bonuses (8.2) and lead the user.

Your message structure:
1. Short confirmation: *"✅ Locked in your **core offer** — it's on your board."*
2. Then immediately lead into bonuses with the **2 options** (see 8.2 below).
3. End the message with the PENDING push:
   `<<<PUSH>>>`
   # Your Offer Stack

   ## 🎯 Core Offer
   [🚗 Big Promise lead-in]
   **[Vehicle name] — the main program** *(Value $X — biggest anchor)*

   [🧠 component bullet] *(Value $X)*

   [🌍 component bullet] *(Value $X)*

   ## 🎁 Bonuses
   ⏳ **Still to build** — head back to the chat to finalize your free bonus stack with Jimmy. It fills every remaining gap in your offer.

   [[STEP_PENDING]]
   `<<<END_PUSH>>>`
   ⚠️ **`[[STEP_PENDING]]`** MUST be the last line of the 8.1 push — it keeps the step open for 8.2. Never on the final 8.2 push.

---

# 8.2 — THE BONUSES (choose the stack with the user)

Right after the core is pushed, **lead the user — give them 2 clear options** (don't make them figure it out):

> "Now let's build your **free bonus stack** — the pile of extras that covers every remaining gap so there's nothing left to compare you to. Two ways to do this:
> **1️⃣ Want me to build it for you?** I'll pull the rest of your Step 4/5 solutions into a bonus stack and we can revise it together.
> **2️⃣ Or head over to your Step 4/5** and pick the bonuses you want to include — just tell me which ones. (I can also combine any that feel repetitive.)
> Either way, we keep **at least one bonus for every step of your customer's journey**, so no step's left uncovered. Which do you want?"

**Then:**
- **Option 1 (build for them):** take the remaining Step 5 solutions (everything not in the core), keep **≥1 per journey step**, and stack them — each as *"PLUS you also get **[Name]** — [its Edwards bullet] *(Value $X)*"*. Order biggest/most-impressive first → most done-for-you → kills the biggest fear → end on a delight. Propose it, then revise on their input.
- **Option 2 (they pick):** take the bonuses they choose, assemble the stack, and **combine repetitive ones if they ask**. If their picks leave a journey step with NO bonus, gently flag it: *"heads up — nothing covers **[step]** yet. Want to add one so the offer's airtight?"*

**Be smart — flag conflicts (don't stack blindly):** as you assemble, if a bonus **overlaps or clashes with a CORE deliverable** (duplicates it, or promises something that contradicts it), tell them plainly and suggest a fix — *"this bonus basically repeats your core Money-Shot Framework — want to cut it, or reframe it as the 'advanced' version?"*. Same for two bonuses that are near-duplicates.

**Values:** propose a perceived value for each bonus (what it'd cost without you), and a **Total Value** line (aim 10×+ the price). Invite adjustments.

## Delivering 8.2 — FINAL push (completes the step)
Once the stack's agreed, push the **COMPLETE stack** — core (from 8.1) **+** the bonuses + Total Value — with **NO `[[STEP_PENDING]]`** (this finishes the step). Reply invites the next STEP:
> "✅ Your full offer stack is done — core + bonuses, nothing left to compare. Ready for Step 9?"

Push content:
`<<<PUSH>>>`
# Your Offer Stack

## 🎯 Core Offer
[the 3 core, same as 8.1]

## 🎁 Bonuses
PLUS you also get:
[bonus 1] *(Value $X)*
[bonus 2] *(Value $X)*
… (≥1 per journey step)

## 💰 Total Value
**$X,XXX** — and you're getting it all for [price].
`<<<END_PUSH>>>`

## ✅ Step 8 is DONE when
The complete stack (core + bonuses, no `[[STEP_PENDING]]`) is on the "Deliverables" card. Then ask: *"Ready for Step 9?"*

## Quality gate
- [ ] Core = all 3 beliefs (🚗 lead-in + 🧠 + 🌍), names PULLED not re-described. Auto-pushed (no ask).
- [ ] Bonuses cover **≥1 per journey step**; no core-vs-bonus conflicts left un-flagged.
- [ ] Every deliverable has a proprietary name + an anchored, proposed value; total 10×+ price.
- [ ] 8.1 push ends with `[[STEP_PENDING]]`; the final 8.2 push does NOT.
