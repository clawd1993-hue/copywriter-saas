# Call-Booker Section 12 SOP — Guarantee / Urgency (the CALL is the risk-reversal)

> **When to load:** Call-Booker VSL Section 12. On a call-booker the risk reversal isn't a refund — it's the FREE, no-obligation call. Urgency = limited call slots.
> **What it does:** make booking feel risk-free (it's a free call, no pressure) + give a real reason to book NOW (limited slots).
> **Output:** a risk-reversal + urgency line, pushed to the "Guarantee/Urgency" card.
> **This does NOT ask about guarantee types** (that's the DTS version). Here the guarantee IS the free call — just write it.

## Opening framing
*"Now Section 12: Guarantee + Urgency. On a call-booker we don't do a refund guarantee — the risk reversal is that the call itself is FREE, zero obligation, no hard pitch. And the urgency is limited call slots. Let me write that."* Then write it.

## What it is
Risk reversal = **the call is free, no obligation, no pressure.** They risk nothing by booking — worst case they get clarity and walk. Urgency = **limited call slots** (real scarcity: you only take so many calls a week).

## How to write it
1. **Reverse the risk = the call:** plain words — *"The call is completely free. No obligation, no hard pitch. Worst case, you walk away with a clear plan. Best case, we figure out if this is a fit."* (Take the fear out of booking.)
2. **Real urgency = limited slots:** *"I only take [X] calls a week, and they fill up."* Never fake — if the number isn't real, keep it honest ("spots are limited").
3. Keep it tight — a couple lines.

## The shape
```
Booking the call costs you nothing. It's free, there's zero obligation, and there's no hard pitch — worst case you leave with a clear next step.

But I only take [X] calls a week and they fill fast — so if that "for you if" list was you, grab a slot now before they're gone.
```

## How to run it (write → push → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat.
- If you don't know the real slot number, ask ONE quick question OR keep it honestly vague ("limited spots"). Don't invent a fake number.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- Visible line: *"Done — your Guarantee + Urgency's in the card (free-call risk reversal + limited slots). Tweak it there, or ready for Section 13 (the CTA)?"*

## Rules
- Risk reversal = **the free, no-obligation call** — NOT a refund guarantee.
- Urgency = **limited call slots**, real (never fake scarcity).
- No price. 3rd-grade. Tight (a couple lines).

## Quality gate
- [ ] Frames the call as free / no obligation / no pressure (removes booking risk).
- [ ] Real urgency = limited slots (not fabricated).
- [ ] No price; tight; flows from Qualify.
