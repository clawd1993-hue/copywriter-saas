# Webinar Section 1 SOP — Hook (Bold Promise) — *Big Domino folded in*

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 1 (the very open of the presentation). Different from the short-VSL Hook — this is the webinar OPEN and carries the Opening Protocol.
> **What it does:** the open. 4 jobs in one segment: (1) the **big promise** that stops them, (2) the **ONE belief** you'll prove all webinar (the **Big Domino** — folded in here, not a separate section), (3) the **frame**, (4) the **stay-to-end retention hook**.
> **Output:** the spoken webinar open, pushed to the "Hook (Bold Promise)" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 3 (biggest desire + pain) + Step 6 (the vehicle) + **the Big Domino** (the ONE belief ← Step 6 🚗 vehicle-belief).

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 1: the Hook — the webinar open. Webinars are won or lost right here. This one segment has to do four things: land the big promise, plant the ONE belief we'll prove all session (the Big Domino), set the frame, and give them a reason to stay to the end. Let me write it."* Then write it.

## ⚠️ "Vehicle" here = the NEW WAY / OPPORTUNITY (the concept) — NOT the branded product name
When this SOP says "the vehicle," it means the **new opportunity / mechanism** — the *new way* to get the result (e.g. "a done-for-you faceless funnel," "a proven system for booking paid shoots"). **Do NOT use the branded product/offer name** (e.g. "The 90-Day Paid Portrait Pro Blueprint") in the Hook — that name is held for the offer reveal (§10 Product Overview / §11 Pitch). The open names the *idea*, not the product. If Step 6 gives you a branded name, strip it down to the underlying new-way concept for the hook.

## The 4 beats, IN ORDER (this is the spoken open)
1. **Welcome + housekeeping** — *"grab a pen, close the tabs, full attention for the next [X] minutes."*
2. **Big promise** — the specific result **via the new way** (the concept, not the brand name) + the top objection dissolved (*"even if [objection]"*). **Specific beats clever.**
3. **The Big Domino** (folded in) — the ONE belief you'll prove: **"[the new way / opportunity] is THE way to [dream], and it's possible even for someone like me."** Frame it as a promise you'll prove — *"here's the one thing that has to be true…"* — but say it **like a real person talking, not a guru catchphrase.** Natural over slick. If you can't say the belief in one sentence, it's not the domino yet.
4. **Stay-to-end hook** — the gift for **everyone who stays live**. *(Biggest lever on webinar watch-time — NEVER skip it.)*

## Grounding (pull from the board — never invent)
- **Big result + top objection** ← Step 3 (biggest desire + pain) + Step 6 (the new-way concept — NOT the brand name).
- **The Big Domino belief** ← Step 6 🚗 vehicle-belief — the ONE belief (the new way is THE way) that, if true, makes the dream inevitable.

## Spoken example (match this voice — conversational, spoken, not written-copy)
> "Welcome — grab a pen and close your tabs. In the next [X] minutes I'll show you how to [big result] using [the new way — the concept, NOT a brand name] — even if [top objection]. And really, it all comes down to one thing: if I can show you today that [Big Domino belief], then [dream] stops being a maybe. That's my whole job today. Stick with me to the end, because I've got [gift] for everyone who stays live."

*(Note the delivery: "if I can show you today…" reads more natural than a slick "if I can prove just one thing." Keep the Big Domino, lose the guru polish.)*

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- Visible line: *"Done — your Hook (the open) is in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 2 (Credibility — your origin story)?"*

## Rules
- Name the **result AND the new way** (the concept). **NOT the branded product/offer name** — that's held for §10/11.
- **Fold in the ONE belief** (the Big Domino) — not a list of beliefs. Say it **naturally, like a person**, not a guru catchphrase.
- **Add the stay-to-end retention line** — it's the single biggest lever on watch-time.
- Everything downstream exists to prove this domino — so it must be crisp; later sections call back to it.
- **Spoken tone** (this is a webinar being said out loud), 3rd-grade words.

## Quality test
Would the avatar cancel what they're doing to keep watching? AND — if they believed *only* the Big Domino, would the sale basically be made? If no to the second, it's the **wrong domino** — rewrite it.

## Quality gate
- [ ] All 4 beats present + in order (welcome/housekeeping → big promise → Big Domino → stay-to-end hook).
- [ ] Names the result AND the new way (concept). **NO branded product/offer name in the hook.**
- [ ] Big Domino = ONE belief (← Step 6 🚗), said naturally (not guru-cheesy); passes the "if they believed only this, the sale's made" test.
- [ ] Stay-to-end retention hook included.
- [ ] Spoken tone, 3rd-grade; hands off into Credibility.
