# Webinar Section 16 SOP — Guarantee & Urgency

> **When to load:** Webinar Section 16 (right after Objection Crush).
> **What it does:** risk reversal + real scarcity — flip the risk buyer→seller, then a true reason to act now.
> **Output:** a named guarantee + real urgency, pushed to the "Guarantee & Urgency" card.
> **Source:** OUTSIDE the system — the user provides the terms. ASK them; never assume.

## This section ASKS first (guarantee type + real urgency). Do NOT push on the first turn.

## Phase 1 — Show the options, let THEM pick (guarantee ONLY, no push)
Open with the framing: *"Now Section 16: Guarantee + Urgency — where we flip the risk onto you and give one real reason to act now. First the guarantee — here are the 4 types, then I'll recommend the fit:"*

List **all 4 briefly** (this is high-ticket, so lean toward Conditional/Performance):
- 🔓 **Unconditional** — "try it, full refund if you don't love it." Strongest, but best for low-ticket/cold.
- ✅ **Conditional / service** — "do [the key actions] and if you don't [result], I'll [keep working with you till you win / refund / 2x]." Filters tire-kickers — **great for high-ticket / done-with-you.**
- 🚫 **Anti-guarantee** — "all sales final" + a reason why. For exclusive / can't-be-unseen.
- 📈 **Performance** — "if I don't perform, I don't get paid." For DWY / revshare.

**Recommend the fit** (high-ticket → Conditional or Performance) + ask: *"Which fits — and give me the terms in your own words?"* NO push, no urgency yet.

## Phase 2 — Get the real urgency
Confirm the guarantee pick, then: *"Now the urgency — what's REAL for you? Price rising / founding price · limited spots · bonus expiry · cohort deadline?"* Handle all-at-once or in parts; don't write until you have both (or "just go").

## Phase 3 — Write it + push
Flip the risk **buyer → seller**, state and **NAME** the guarantee plainly, then the real urgency + the reason it exists.
- Direct-push once written; don't reprint in chat.
- End handing into the Re-Stack: *"So here's everything you get when you join today…"*
- Visible line: *"Done — your Guarantee + Urgency's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 17 (the Re-Stack)?"*

## Rules
- **Show all 4, recommend the fit, ASK** — never assume the type.
- **NAME the guarantee** (named ones convert better).
- Guarantee must **genuinely de-risk** (risk sits on the seller).
- Urgency must be **REAL**, never fake.
- Hand into the Re-Stack.
- Spoken tone, 3rd-grade.

## Quality test
Does the risk now sit **entirely on the seller**, and is the urgency something that's actually true?

## Quality gate
- [ ] Showed all 4 types + recommended the high-ticket fit + asked (didn't assume).
- [ ] Guarantee is NAMED and genuinely de-risks (buyer→seller).
- [ ] Urgency is REAL (their true reason), not fabricated.
- [ ] Hands into the Re-Stack; spoken tone, 3rd-grade.
