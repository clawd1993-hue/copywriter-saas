# Call-Booker Section 10 SOP — Bonuses (NO PRICE / NO TOTAL — hand into Qualify)

> **When to load:** Call-Booker VSL Section 10. Same bonus stack as the DTS version — EXCEPT no price reveal, and it hands into Qualify (not the close).
> **What it does:** stack every bonus (each crushes an objection = another reason to book). Kill the price/total reveal.
> **Pulls from:** Step 8.2 bonus stack + vehicle name.

## Opening framing
*"Now the Bonuses. On a call-booker these do double duty — every bonus crushes an objection, which is another reason they'll book the call. We stack them all, but we DON'T do the price/total reveal — that's for the call."* Then write it.

## Same as the DTS Bonuses — with these changes
- Open with the **limited + free** hook ("for the next [X] who book/join…") and stack the bonuses (name + benefit + value) from **Step 8.2** (never raw Step 5).
- **KILL the ending price/total reveal.** No "add it all up = $X" and no "you get everything for $Y." **No grand-total math, no price.** (That's the call's job.)
- Values on individual bonuses are fine (they build worth) — just **never sum them into a total-vs-price** and never state the offer price.
- **Hand off into Qualify** (Section 11), not into a close — e.g. end on *"…and here's who this is actually for."*

## How to run it (write → push → tweak or move on)
Editable card, direct-push, don't reprint in chat.
- PUSH wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>` (short confirmation first).
- Visible line: *"Done — your Bonus stack's in the card (no price/total — that's for the call). Tweak it there, or ready for Section 11 (Who This Is For)?"*

## Rules
- Bonuses from Step 8.2 (not raw Step 5); limited + free framing.
- **NO grand total, NO price, NO value-vs-price math.**
- Hand off into Qualify, not a close.
- 3rd-grade.
