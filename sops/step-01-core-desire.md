# Step 1 SOP — Pick the Core Desire (Market + Buying Drivers)

> **When to load:** At Step 1 of the Offer Creation Engine — the very start.
> **What this step does:** Lock which of the 3 evergreen markets the offer lives in, and flag which of Jim Edwards' 10 Reasons People Buy it hits — the emotional drivers that fuel the copy later.
> **Output:** Core market (Health / Wealth / Relationships) + the 2–3 buying drivers the offer hits hardest.
> **Prerequisite:** None — this is the start.

---

## What Is Step 1?

Every offer lives in one of the **3 evergreen markets** — the things humans always spend money on:

- ❤️ **Health** — the body & mind (feel better, look better, live longer, escape pain)
- 💰 **Wealth** — money & time (make more, keep more, work less)
- 👥 **Relationships** — love, status & belonging (be loved, be respected, be seen)

Pick the ONE your offer **primarily** serves. Most offers touch more than one — pick the dominant market; the others become secondary angles.

---

## The 10 Reasons People Buy (Jim Edwards, *Copywriting Secrets*) — grouped by market

The 3 markets are the **buckets.** Edwards' **10 Reasons People Buy** are the specific **emotional drivers** inside them. Once you've picked the market, flag which drivers your offer hits — that's the raw fuel for the Desire and Pitch sections downstream.

**💰 Wealth**
1. Make more money
2. Save money
3. Save time
4. Avoid effort

**❤️ Health**
5. Escape physical or mental pain
6. Get more comfort
7. Improve health / hygiene

**👥 Relationships**
8. Gain praise
9. Be loved
10. Increase status / popularity

> ⚠️ **A few are cross-cutting.** "Save time," "avoid effort," "more comfort," and "escape pain" can apply in *any* market (an AI tool "saves time" for a Wealth offer; therapy "escapes pain" in Health). Group by the dominant market, but use any driver that genuinely applies. **Edwards' rule: tie MULTIPLE reasons to the buy, not just one.**

---

## How to Run It (with the user) — always do this, in order

1. **Write out the menu.** Show the user the 3 markets + the 10 reasons grouped under them (the list above) — so they can see their options laid out.
2. **Ask the question:** **_"What is the core desire your offer helps your customer achieve?"_**
3. **From their answer, lock:** the core market (Health / Wealth / Relationships) + the 2–3 of the 10 drivers the offer hits hardest.
4. Write it down: `Core market: [X] · Drivers: #, #, #`.

**Quality test:** Can you name at least 2 of the 10 reasons your offer delivers? If only one — either the offer is thin, or you haven't found its other angles yet.

## Example — 🎬 The AI Copywriting Tool (VSL Workshop)

- **Drivers hit:** #1 Make more money (better copy → more sales) · #3 Save time (writes it in minutes) · #4 Avoid effort (no learning to write, no hiring a copywriter)
