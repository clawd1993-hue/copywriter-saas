# VSL Section 10 SOP — Bonuses

> **When to load:** VSL Section 10 (after the Pitch).
> **What it does:** stack the free gifts on top of the core offer, then do the total-value math.
> **Output:** the bonus stack (each = name + benefit + value) opened with a limited/free hook, closed with the value math, pushed to the "Bonuses" card.
> **Pulls from:** Step 8.2 — the curated bonus stack (NEVER raw Step 5).

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
*"Now Section 10: the Bonuses. Section 9 revealed the core offer — this stacks the free gifts on top to make it a no-brainer, then adds it all up against the price. And we frame it as limited and free — 'for the next handful of people who join.'"* Then write it.

## What it is
The free gifts they get when they join today. Each was already selected and packaged in Step 8.2 (and still traces to a specific Step 4 problem). Section 9 revealed the core product (Step 8.1); this stacks the bonuses on top.

## The limited + free hook (open the stack with this — Michael's ask)
Open the pushed bonus block with a **limited-quantity + free** line, e.g.:
> "And for the next **[30] members** who join today, I'm also throwing in these bonuses — **completely free**:"

- Frame it as **scarce** (a limited number of people / spots) AND **free** (a gift on top, not part of the price).
- Use a believable number as a placeholder (default ~"next 30 members" / "first 50") and note in the chat that they can set the exact number — it's their scarcity lever. (The deeper urgency mechanism lives in Section 11; here it just makes the bonuses feel like a now-or-never gift.)

## Grounding (pull from Step 8.2 — never re-pick from raw Step 5)
- Take the bonuses **exactly as packaged in Step 8.2** — the selection/curation already happened there.
- ⚠️ **Why 8.2, not raw Step 5:** Step 8.2 already (a) excluded anything used as a core deliverable in 8.1 (no bonus duplicates the core), and (b) excluded items reserved for paid upsells. Re-pulling from raw Step 5 re-introduces both bugs.
- Use the **vehicle name** ← Step 6 in the intro ("when you join [Vehicle Name] today…").
- Pull the **core values** from Section 9 (the VSL so far) — the valued component pieces (🧠 + 🌍). The flagship roadmap carries no value; just sum whatever "(Value $X)" figures are actually on the board — don't assume how many.

## The format (VSL-tight — name + one benefit + value, NOT the full 5-part bullet)
```
And for the next [30] members who join [Vehicle Name] today, you're also getting these bonuses — completely free:

🎁 **[Bonus 1 Name]** — [one-line benefit], so you can [outcome]. (Value: $X)
🎁 **[Bonus 2 Name]** — [one-line benefit], so you can [outcome]. (Value: $X)
🎁 **[Bonus 3 Name]** — [one-line benefit], so you can [outcome]. (Value: $X)
[...however many Step 8.2 contains]

Add it all up — the core training plus every bonus — that's over **$[grand total]** in value.
And today, you get everything for just **$[price]**.
```
*(When a bonus maps to ONE journey step, you can note the step in brackets after the name. For curated/combined bonuses spanning multiple steps, skip the brackets — don't force it.)*

## Total-value math (do this right)
- **Grand total = the core component values (🧠 + 🌍, from Section 9 / Step 8.1) PLUS the Step 8.2 bonuses.** The flagship roadmap carries no value — don't invent one. Add up exactly the "(Value $X)" figures on the board — however many there are — and anchor THAT grand total against the price. Never anchor with the bonus value alone.
- If the **price is known**, use it. If not (it's set in Section 12), leave a clear **[your price]** placeholder and tell them in the chat you've left it for the CTA step.

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → tweak or move on)
The Bonuses card is **editable**, so DON'T ask permission — write it and push straight to the card. Per the section output contract, do NOT reprint the stack in the chat.
1. Open with the framing above (a line or two).
2. Write the limited/free hook + the bonus stack + the value math.
3. **Immediately PUSH it** wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>`. Short confirmation FIRST (visible), then the markers.
4. Visible line — mention you framed the bonuses as limited + free (and they can set the exact number/price), then the fork: *"Done — your Bonus stack's in the card. Tweak it there, or ready for Section 11 (Guarantee & Urgency)?"*

## Rules
- **Count = whatever Step 8.2 contains** (NOT fixed at any number).
- Frame as **"free when you join today"** — not part of the core product.
- **Limited** — open with the scarcity + free hook.
- Each bonus: name + one clear benefit + a believable-but-impressive value.
- The core component deliverables (Section 9 — the valued 🧠/🌍 bullets) are separate from these bonuses, but BOTH count toward the grand total. Sum what's actually on the board.
- 3rd-grade words.

## Quality test
Does the stack of bonuses alone feel worth more than the price — and does "for the next [X] members, free" make them want to move NOW? If the bonuses feel like filler, pick different ones (from 8.2); if the scarcity feels flat, sharpen the number/framing.
