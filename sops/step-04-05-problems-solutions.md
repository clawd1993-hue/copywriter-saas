# Step 4/5 SOP — Problems → Solutions (merged bubble)

> **When to load:** Step 4/5 (the merged "Problems & Solutions" bubble), once Customer Research (Step 3) is on the board.
> **What this step does:** Map the customer's journey → for each journey step, surface 4 problems (Value-Equation lenses) → turn each problem into a named solution. The pile of solutions IS the product.
> **Output on the board:** ONE combined map — per journey step: each of the 4 lens-problems immediately paired with its named solution.

Run it in TWO turns: first lock the journey (Part A), then build + push the whole map in one go (Part B). **Only ONE push happens, at the very end** — never push a problems-only version first (that makes the app think the step is finished and skip ahead).

---

## PART A — Lock the customer journey (fast, collaborative, NO push)
List the sequential steps the customer must take to get from "just bought / decided" to the result.
- **Propose them yourself** from what you already know (their Core Desire, Market, and the real Customer Research on the board). Give **5–6 steps**, short and sequential.
- Ask ONE quick confirm: *"Here's the journey I'd map for your customer — look right, or want to tweak any?"* Take their edits. **Do not push anything yet.** This is the one spot you need their nod.

Once they confirm, go straight into building the full map (Part B) — that's the next thing you do.

## PART B — Build the FULL map (problems + solutions together) and push ONCE
For EVERY journey step, produce all 4 lens-problems, and **immediately pair each problem with its named solution.** Generate the whole thing, then AUTO-PUSH it (see Push rules). Keep your chat message short.

### The 4 lenses — keep them DISTINCT (this is the whole skill)
Each problem is the customer's fear in their OWN voice — pull the language from the real Step 3 research on the board (objections, failures, fears); don't invent it.
1. 🎯 **Dream Outcome** — "Even if I SUCCEED at this step, the result still won't be what I actually want." Doubt about the DESTINATION. Pattern: *"Even if I [do the step], [the result] still won't be [good enough]."* Think status.
2. 🎯 **Likelihood** — "Can *someone like me* actually pull this off?" Doubt about the PERSON — identity, skill, age, background, past failures.
3. ⏰ **Time Delay** — "How long will this take? I need results now / I'm too busy." Doubt about the TIMELINE.
4. 😩 **Effort & Sacrifice** — "How hard, confusing, exhausting, or expensive is this?" Doubt about the DIFFICULTY.

**⚠️ #1 mistake: a Likelihood fear disguised as Dream Outcome.** "Will this work? / Can I do it?" = Likelihood. "Even if it works, the payoff is hollow" = Dream Outcome. Self-check every Dream-Outcome line: does it start from *succeeding* and still feel let down?

### The named solution (Edwards' bullet formula) — one per problem
> **[Named Solution]** — [what it is] **so you can** [the specific outcome that solves THIS problem] **even if** [their real objection, pulled from Step 3] **which means** [the deeper emotional / identity / status payoff].
- **Name** = branded + specific ("The Freeze-Proof Opener", not "a guide"). The/A + descriptive name.
- **What it is** = a deliverable: guide, playbook, template, script, toolkit, protocol, system, quiz, diagnostic, checklist, framework, vault.
- **so you can** = solves the SPECIFIC problem it's paired with. **even if** = their REAL Step-3 objection. **which means** = emotional/identity/status payoff, second person ("you").

### Output format (so the card reads clean) — follow this exactly
Group by journey step. Under each step, list the 4 lenses; under each fear, its paired solution with a `➜`. Use this shape:

```
## Step 1 — [step name]

🎯 Dream Outcome — "[the fear, in their words]"
➜ **[Named Solution]** — [what it is] so you can [outcome] even if [objection] which means [payoff].

🎯 Likelihood — "[the fear]"
➜ **[Named Solution]** — …

⏰ Time — "[the fear]"
➜ **[Named Solution]** — …

😩 Effort — "[the fear]"
➜ **[Named Solution]** — …

---
```
(Repeat the block for every journey step, `---` divider between steps.)

**Worked example (one lens, paired):**
> 🎯 Dream Outcome — "Even if I finally learn manual mode, I'll still just be a guy with a camera who knows his settings — technically correct photos nobody stops to look at."
> ➜ **The Scroll-Stopper Framework** — a shot-and-story system so you can take photos people actually stop and stare at, even if you're self-taught and feel like just a hobbyist, which means you're finally the photographer people remember, not the guy with a nice camera.

---

## 🚀 Push rules — AUTO-PUSH ONCE (overrides the global "always ask before pushing" rule)
- This is a big structured output — it belongs on the board, not dumped in chat.
- **Do NOT push a problems-only version.** Build the COMPLETE map (every step's 4 fears, each with its solution) and push that, one time, at the end. (A problems-only push would fill the card and make the app advance past this step before the solutions exist — that's a bug, never do it.)
- Keep your visible chat message SHORT, e.g.: *"✅ Done — your full problem → solution map is on the board. Every fear your customer has at every step now has a named solution, and that stack of solutions is basically your product. Have a look, and say the word when you're ready for Step 6."*
- AUTO-PUSH the full content (no "want me to push?"): end your message with
  `<<<PUSH>>>`
  [the complete combined map]
  `<<<END_PUSH>>>`

## ✅ Step 4/5 is DONE when
The single combined map (journey steps × 4 lens-problems, each paired with its named solution) is on the "Problems & Solutions" card, and your short chat confirmation invites them to review and move to Step 6.

## Quality gate (check before you push)
- [ ] Every Dream-Outcome line starts from *succeeding* and still feels let down (not a disguised "will it work?").
- [ ] Likelihood = about the person; Time = waiting; Effort = doing. Distinct.
- [ ] Every solution uses the full "so you can… even if… which means…" formula, branded name, second person.
- [ ] Every "even if" uses REAL language from the Step 3 research.
- [ ] Every fear is paired with exactly one solution. Problems-only was NOT pushed.
