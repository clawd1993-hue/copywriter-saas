# Step 4/5 SOP — Problems → Solutions (merged bubble)

> **When to load:** Step 4/5 (the merged "Problems & Solutions" bubble), once Customer Research (Step 3) is on the board.
> **What this step does:** Map the customer's journey → for each journey step, surface 4 problems (Value-Equation lenses) → turn each problem into a named solution. The pile of solutions IS the product.
> **Output on the board:** A structured map — per journey step: 4 lens-problems, each paired with its named solution.

This step has TWO parts (Problems, then Solutions). Run them as two passes. Both land on the same "Problems & Solutions" card.

---

## PART A — Lock the customer journey (fast, collaborative)
List the sequential steps the customer must take to get from "just bought / decided" to the result.
- **Propose them yourself** from what you already know (their Core Desire, Market, and the real Customer Research on the board). Give **5–6 steps**, short and sequential. Example shape: "1) Understand the model → 2) Pick a niche → 3) Create the product → 4) Build the funnel → 5) Drive traffic → 6) Get the first sale."
- Ask ONE quick confirm: *"Here's the journey I'd map for your customer — look right, or want to tweak any?"* Take their edits, then move on. Don't interrogate. This is the one spot you need their nod before generating.

## PART B — The problem map (4 Value-Equation lenses per step)
For EVERY journey step, write exactly 4 problems — one per lens. These are the customer's fears in their OWN voice (pull the language from the real Step 3 research on the board — objections, failures, fears; don't invent it).

**The 4 lenses — keep them DISTINCT (this is the whole skill):**
1. 🎯 **Dream Outcome** — "Even if I SUCCEED at this step, the result still won't be what I actually want." Doubt about the DESTINATION, not the journey. Pattern: *"Even if I [do the step], [the result] still won't be [good enough / what I want]."* Think status: if nailing the step doesn't raise their status, that's the Dream-Outcome fear.
2. 🎯 **Likelihood** — "Can *someone like me* actually pull this off?" Doubt about the PERSON — their identity, skill, age, background, past failures, situation.
3. ⏰ **Time Delay** — "How long will this take? I need results now / I'm too busy." Doubt about the TIMELINE.
4. 😩 **Effort & Sacrifice** — "How hard, confusing, exhausting, or expensive is this?" Doubt about the DIFFICULTY / what they must give up.

**⚠️ The #1 mistake: writing a Likelihood fear under Dream Outcome.** "Will this work?" / "Can I do it?" = Likelihood. "Even if it works, the payoff is hollow" = Dream Outcome. Self-check every Dream-Outcome line: does it start from *succeeding* and still feel let down? If it's really "will it work," it's Likelihood — fix it.

**Worked example — one step, all 4 lenses (💰 side income — "Launch your first offer"):**
1. 🎯 Dream Outcome: "Even if I launch, a few hundred bucks won't change my life — still stuck at my job."
2. 🎯 Likelihood: "I'm not techy or salesy. Mine'll flop while others crush it."
3. ⏰ Time: "Building a whole offer = months I don't have after work."
4. 😩 Effort: "Website, payments, content — a pile of stuff on top of a 9-5."

**Delivering Part B:** Don't dump all ~20 problems as a wall of text in chat. Generate the full map and AUTO-PUSH it to the board (see Push rules). Your chat message is short: *"✅ Your problem map's on the board — [N] journey steps, 4 fears each, in your customer's own words. Take a look. Say the word and I'll turn every one of these into a named solution."*

## PART C — Named solutions (Edwards' bullet formula)
Take each problem from Part B and write a named solution. Every solution uses this formula, no shortcuts:

> **[Named Solution]** — [what it is] **so you can** [the specific outcome that solves THIS problem] **even if** [their real objection, pulled from Step 3] **which means** [the deeper emotional / identity / status payoff].

- **Name** = branded + specific ("The Freeze-Proof Opener", not "a guide"). Use The/A + descriptive name.
- **What it is** = a deliverable format: guide, playbook, template, script, toolkit, protocol, system, quiz, diagnostic, checklist, framework, vault.
- **so you can** = solves the SPECIFIC Part-B problem it maps to (not a vague benefit).
- **even if** = their REAL objection from the Step 3 research (not made up).
- **which means** = emotional/identity/status payoff — what changes in their LIFE. Written in second person ("you").

**Example (full formula):** *"**The Habit-Swap System** — a set of one-for-one daily food swaps so you can drop fat without counting a single calorie, even if you've got zero willpower, which means the weight comes off without you living in 'diet jail.'"*

**Delivering Part C:** Generate a named solution for every problem, then AUTO-PUSH the **COMBINED** map — every journey step showing its 4 problems each paired with its named solution — to the board. (The push replaces the card, so this final push must contain BOTH the problems and their solutions, not solutions alone.) Short chat message: *"✅ Done — every fear on your board now has a named solution. That stack of solutions is basically your product. Have a look, and say the word when you're ready for Step 6."*

---

## 🚀 Push rules for this step (AUTO-PUSH — overrides the global "always ask before pushing" rule)
The outputs here are big and structured — they belong on the board, not dumped in chat. For BOTH Part B and Part C: generate the output, keep your visible chat message to the SHORT confirmation above, and AUTO-PUSH the full content by ending the message with:
`<<<PUSH>>>`
[the full content — Part B: the problem map · Part C: the COMBINED problems+solutions map]
`<<<END_PUSH>>>`
No "want me to push?" question — push automatically once the content is ready (same as Steps 1–3 landing their output on the board). Never paste the full map into the visible chat.

## ✅ Step 4/5 is DONE when
Part A (journey confirmed) + Part B (4-lens problem map) + Part C (a named solution per problem) are all on the "Problems & Solutions" card, and your short chat confirmation invites them to review and move to Step 6.

## Quality gate (check before you push Part C)
- [ ] Every Dream-Outcome line starts from *succeeding* and still feels let down (not a disguised "will it work?").
- [ ] Every Likelihood line is specific to the customer's identity/situation.
- [ ] Time = waiting; Effort = doing. They're distinct.
- [ ] Every solution uses the full "so you can… even if… which means…" formula.
- [ ] Every "even if" uses REAL language from the Step 3 research (not invented).
- [ ] Every "which means" hits an emotional/identity/status payoff, in second person.
- [ ] Every solution has a branded, specific name.
