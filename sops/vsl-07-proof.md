# VSL Section 7 SOP — Proof

> **When to load:** VSL Section 7 (after Credibility).
> **What it does:** the "it's not just me" proof — 3 believable testimonials that show real people got the result.
> **Output:** 3 placeholder testimonials, pushed straight to the "Proof" card.
> **Approach:** DON'T interview. Auto-write 3 realistic placeholders, push them, and tell the user to swap in their real ones.

## Opening framing (say this, in your own words)
Open on the transition out of Credibility: *"Now Section 7: Proof. Section 6 was your story — this is the 'and it's not just me' part. This is where other people back up that it works."* Then write it — don't ask them for testimonials, just generate believable placeholders (most people don't have them polished yet, and blank proof kills a VSL).

## What it is
Testimonials / results from real people — the "it's not just HIM" proof. Because most users won't have these ready, you write **realistic placeholders**, clearly marked to swap, so the VSL is never missing its proof.

## How to write the 3 placeholders (grounded — never hypey)
Write **3 testimonials**. Format each:
> **[Name], [avatar descriptor]** — "[relatable starting point] → [specific result + timeframe], [quick tie to the mechanism]."

- **Ground them in the real avatar** (← Step 3) and the mechanism (← the Vehicle/Method) — the starting points should mirror where the reader is now.
- **Lead with the most RELATABLE result, not the most extreme.**
- **Cover a range of avatars** — each one quietly kills a different objection (no time / not techy / never sold before / tried and failed).
- **Believable, not hypey** — specific numbers ("$4k month," "first sale in 2 weeks"), never "$1M in 3 days."
- Starting point = relatable, result = aspirational.

## How to run it (write → push straight to the card → reassure → move on)
The Proof card is **editable**, so DON'T ask permission — write the 3 placeholders and push them straight to the card. *(Overrides the usual "always ask" rule.)*
1. Open with the framing above (a line or two).
2. Write the 3 placeholder testimonials.
3. **Immediately PUSH them** to the card, wrapped in `<<<PUSH>>>` … `<<<END_PUSH>>>`. Short confirmation FIRST (visible), then the markers.
4. Visible line — the reassurance (say it in your own warm words, this vibe): *"Done — I dropped in 3 placeholder testimonials. Swap them for your real clients' results whenever you've got them — just keep the focus on the **outcome** they got, not the effort. And if you don't have any yet, don't stress: testimonials aren't the only proof. Copy this sharp — that reads the reader's mind — is proof all on its own; it makes them stop and think 'okay, this person actually knows what they're talking about.'"* Then the fork: *"Tweak them in the card, or ready for Section 8 (Product Overview)?"*

### Push content format (renders clean on the card)
```
⚠️ Placeholder testimonials — swap for your real client results.

[Name], [avatar] — "[starting point] → [result + timeframe], [tie to mechanism]."

[Name], [avatar] — "[starting point] → [result + timeframe], [tie to mechanism]."

[Name], [avatar] — "[starting point] → [result + timeframe], [tie to mechanism]."
```

## Rules
- Write **3** (the card can hold more later, but 3 is the clean default).
- **Specific > vague** ("$34k month" beats "great program!").
- **Believable, not hypey** — no "$1M in 3 days."
- Each = relatable starting point + aspirational result; cover a range of avatars/objections.
- Mark them clearly as placeholders to swap.
- 3rd-grade words; sound like a real person wrote them, not marketing.

## Quality test
Would a skeptical reader believe a real person wrote each one — and see themselves in at least one starting point? If any reads hypey or generic, rewrite it tighter and more specific.

## Quality gate
- [ ] Opens on the "it's not just me" transition from Credibility.
- [ ] Exactly 3 placeholders, clearly marked to swap.
- [ ] Each has a relatable starting point + specific believable result + a tie to the mechanism.
- [ ] Range of avatars, each killing a different objection.
- [ ] Chat reassures: swap for real ones, focus on outcomes, and good copy is proof too if they have none.
